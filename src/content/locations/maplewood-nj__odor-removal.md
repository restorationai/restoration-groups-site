## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Maplewood

We start by identifying the source material, not just the room. Plaster, subfloor, insulation, and ductwork all hold odor differently, and treating the air without treating the material means the smell comes back within days. For basements affected by storm flooding or standing water near Memorial Park and the lower streets around the village, we typically pull affected porous material first, then apply hydroxyl deodorization, which is safe to run in occupied homes and doesn't require residents or pets to leave. For smoke odor from a kitchen or fireplace event in older plaster homes, thermal fogging gets a deodorizing agent into the same cracks and seams that smoke particles traveled through, matching the pattern of the contamination instead of just masking it. Ozone treatment has its place for unoccupied properties with heavy, stubborn odor loads, but we don't run it in homes where people or pets are present, since it requires the space to be vacated during treatment.
