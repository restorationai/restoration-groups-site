## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Plainfield

Plainfield's housing stock runs heavily Victorian and Queen Anne era, built with masonry or wood-frame construction over full basements, many of them later finished for living space. Water intrusion in these homes often travels further and sits longer than in newer construction, because finished basements trap moisture behind drywall and flooring rather than letting it evaporate.
