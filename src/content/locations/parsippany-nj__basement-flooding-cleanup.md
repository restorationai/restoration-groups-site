## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Parsippany

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or submersible pumps depending on depth and access, then move to moisture mapping. ## Equipment & Methods We Use for Basement Flooding

Depth and contamination level drive the equipment choice more than anything else. Clean groundwater intrusion from a cracked foundation calls for air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run on a drying chamber basis, while backup from a cast-iron drain line gets treated as a contaminated loss with containment and more aggressive extraction before drying equipment ever goes in. Thermal imaging and penetrating moisture meters tell us when a slab or stud bay is still holding water the eye can't see, which matters most in the Troy Hills and Lake Hiawatha tracts where finished basements hide a lot of the structure behind drywall.
