## Why Woodbridge Properties See Sewage Backups

Much of Woodbridge Township was built out in waves of capes, split-levels, and colonials from the 1950s through the 1970s, and the cast-iron and clay sewer laterals under those streets are now decades past their intended service life. Tree roots, grease buildup, and plain old corrosion narrow those lines until a hard rain or a holiday's worth of extra flushing is enough to send wastewater back up through basement drains and laundry hookups. Low-lying sections near the Rahway River and the Woodbridge River, especially around Sewaren, Port Reading, and parts of Fords, see an added problem: when the township's combined storm and sanitary infrastructure gets overwhelmed during coastal storms or heavy nor'easters, municipal sewage can back up into private laterals even in homes with no interior plumbing issue at all. Commercial properties along the Route 1 & 9 corridor and near Woodbridge Center mall carry their own risk, with grease-heavy restaurant lines and older retail plumbing that can fail overnight and go undiscovered until the next business day.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Woodbridge

We start by identifying the category of contamination, since a backup from a cracked lateral reads differently than one pushed back by municipal surcharge, and that distinction drives how much flooring, drywall, and insulation has to come out. Standing wastewater and saturated porous materials are removed first, then we extract remaining moisture and begin antimicrobial treatment of framing, subfloor, and any contents that can be salvaged. Air scrubbers and negative air setups keep aerosolized contaminants from migrating to upper floors, which matters in the many split-level Woodbridge homes where the basement sits only a half-flight from the main living space. Every surface that contacted contaminated water is cleaned and sanitized to the IICRC S500/S540 category 3 standard before we run final moisture checks, because a reading that looks dry on the surface can still be carrying contamination in the subfloor.
