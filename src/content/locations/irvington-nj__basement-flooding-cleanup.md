## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Irvington

We start by identifying the source, whether it's groundwater intrusion, a failed sump system, or a supply line break, because that changes how we approach drying. Extraction comes first: truck-mounted or portable pumps pull standing water while we check for secondary pooling behind finished walls, a common issue in the semi-finished basements typical of Irvington's rowhouse layouts. From there we pull baseboards and, where needed, the bottom few inches of drywall to let trapped moisture escape, then set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's size and the concrete's moisture reading rather than on a fixed timer. Drying is tracked daily with moisture meters until readings hit dry-standard, following IICRC S500 guidelines, not guesswork.
