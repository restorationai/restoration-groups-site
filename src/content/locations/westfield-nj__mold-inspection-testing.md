## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Westfield

We start with a visual and moisture-mapping walkthrough, paying particular attention to basement mechanical rooms, the band joist area where old foundations meet newer framing, and any wall that backs up to a bathroom or kitchen plumbing chase. In pre-1930s homes we probe plaster carefully rather than assuming drywall behavior, since plaster can mask surface dryness while the lath behind it stays damp. Where visual and moisture evidence suggests a problem, we collect air samples and, when surfaces are involved, physical samples, and send them to an independent lab for species identification and spore count. Results typically take a few business days, and we walk homeowners through what the numbers mean in plain language, not just a lab report full of Latin names. If remediation is warranted, we document the finding thoroughly, since original trim, plaster, and millwork in these homes often need special handling to protect and match during any repair work that follows.
