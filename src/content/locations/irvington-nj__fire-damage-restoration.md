## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Irvington

We start with a damage assessment that separates structural char and water used in suppression from smoke and soot that has traveled beyond the fire's origin. In Irvington's older multifamily buildings, that usually means checking cockloft and shared wall cavities even if the fire stayed confined to one unit. Contents go through inventory and either cleaning on-site or pack-out for off-site restoration depending on soot penetration. Structural drying follows if firefighting water soaked plaster, subfloor, or framing, since wet plaster in these prewar homes holds moisture far longer than drywall and needs monitored drying, not a quick pass with a fan. Odor treatment, including thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment depending on severity, comes last, after the source materials are cleaned or removed, because treating odor before addressing porous charred material just masks the problem temporarily.
