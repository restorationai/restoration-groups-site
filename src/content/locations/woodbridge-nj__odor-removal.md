## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The right method depends on the odor's chemistry and the building's use. Ozone treatment oxidizes smoke and fire-related compounds effectively but needs a sealed, unoccupied space, so it's a better fit for vacant units, storage areas, or commercial spaces near Woodbridge Center mall during off-hours. Hydroxyl deodorization produces similar oxidizing action without the health restrictions, making it our default for occupied single-family homes. Thermal fogging bridges both methods by matching the particle size of smoke or odor residue, allowing the deodorizer to reach the same surfaces the original odor penetrated, including inside wall cavities and under cabinetry. We pair all of this with air quality monitoring so you get a read on when the space is actually clear, not just when it smells better to a human nose.
