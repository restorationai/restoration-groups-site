## Why Clifton Properties See Sewage Backups

A lot of Clifton's capes, colonials, and split-levels went up in the 1940s through 60s on finished basements, and many still run original cast-iron drain lines that are now reaching the end of their service life. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out, narrowing the pipe until roots, grease, or ordinary debris cause a backup that pushes raw sewage up through the lowest drain in the house, usually a basement floor drain or laundry standpipe. In Lakeview and Delawanna, that problem compounds during heavy rain: both sections sit low relative to the Passaic River, and when the river rises, as it did during Irene and Ida, municipal sewer lines can surcharge and send wastewater backward into connected homes faster than a resident can react. Botany Village's older brick multi-family buildings add another variable, shared lateral lines serving multiple units, which means a clog or break in one apartment's line can surface in a neighbor's basement with no warning.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Clifton

We start by identifying the contamination category before touching anything, since a sewer backup is treated as black water under IICRC guidelines regardless of how clean it looks. Crews extract standing sewage, remove and bag porous materials that can't be sanitized (drywall, carpet pad, insulation), and pull baseboards to check for wicking into wall cavities, which is common in Clifton's older finished basements where furring strips sit directly against block foundation walls. Once the space is cleared, we apply EPA-registered disinfectant, run air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's moisture load, and verify dryness with moisture meters before any rebuild work begins. In two-family rental properties, which are dense throughout Clifton, we coordinate access and documentation separately for each unit so the landlord and tenant both have a clear record of what was affected.

## Clifton Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is often a separate rider from standard water damage coverage, and we see confusion about this constantly in Clifton, especially in homes near Route 21 and the industrial lots along the river where backups tend to cluster after storm events. We document the source of the loss, whether it's a municipal line surcharge, a private lateral failure, or a sump or ejector pump malfunction, because that distinction frequently determines which policy section applies. For rental and multi-family properties in Botany Village and similar sections, we provide separate photo and moisture logs per unit so property managers can route claims to the correct responsible party without delay.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Sewage Cleanup

Black water work calls for different equipment than a clean-water loss. We use truck-mounted extraction for standing waste, HEPA air scrubbers to manage airborne contaminants during demolition, and antimicrobial application equipment sized to the affected square footage rather than a single all-purpose sprayer. Cast-iron drain failures sometimes mean we're cleaning up the same space twice if the underlying line isn't repaired, so we document pipe condition during the job and flag it clearly for the homeowner or their plumber.
