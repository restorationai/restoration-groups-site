## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Clark

The work starts with securing the structure, boarding broken windows or a compromised roofline, and tarping any opening the fire department had to create for ventilation. From there, we separate what can be cleaned from what has to go: soot-stained drywall and insulation usually can't be saved, but trim, flooring, and contents often respond to the right cleaning method. Smoke odor is the part homeowners underestimate most. Protein smoke from a kitchen fire behaves differently than the heavier soot from a structure fire that reached framing lumber, and treating it wrong just pushes the smell back into the air a few weeks later. We pull HVAC filters and inspect ductwork early, since a fire anywhere in a Clark split-level will usually pump some smoke through the return air system even if the fire itself stayed contained to one room.
