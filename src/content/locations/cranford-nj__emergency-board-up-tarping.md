## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Cranford

We start by securing the structure: broken glass and splintered framing get cleared, and openings are measured for exterior-grade plywood cut on site rather than pre-sized panels, because older Cranford homes rarely have standard window or door dimensions. Plywood is fastened through the original framing or masonry, not just the trim, so it holds through sustained wind rather than popping loose. For roofs, we install reinforced poly sheeting rated for extended outdoor exposure, battened down with furring strips rather than just screws through the tarp, which matters on the steeper gable and gambrel roofs common on Cranford West and Lincoln Park East capes. Every opening and every tarp placement is photographed before and after, with measurements noted, because Cranford homeowners who have been through Floyd, Irene, or Ida know that an adjuster is going to ask for documentation and a vague description does not hold up.
