## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Bloomfield

We start with a walkthrough to separate dry smoke residue (brittle, powdery, typical of fast-burning fires) from wet smoke residue (sticky, smeared, typical of smoldering fires), because they require different cleaning agents and the wrong one can push soot deeper into plaster. We then work room by room with HEPA vacuuming, chemical sponges for dry soot, and wet-cleaning agents calibrated to pH-sensitive finishes, followed by thermal fogging or ozone treatment for odor once surfaces are clean, never before, since masking odor over active residue just delays the real problem. Contents go through a separate cleaning and deodorization track rather than staying in place while the structure dries.
