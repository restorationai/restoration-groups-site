## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in East Brunswick

We start by identifying the contamination category, since raw sewage and septic overflow are handled as Category 3 loss under IICRC S500 guidelines regardless of how clean the water looks once it recedes. Standing waste and saturated porous materials, carpet, carpet pad, drywall below the flood line, are removed rather than dried, because those materials can't be sanitized back to a safe condition. What remains gets cleaned, disinfected, and dried with the same monitoring approach we use on any water loss, moisture readings in the framing and subfloor, not just the surface. In East Brunswick's older cast-iron-drain homes, we also check whether the backup originated from a township main or the home's own lateral, since that distinction affects both the cleanup scope and who ultimately pays for the line repair.
