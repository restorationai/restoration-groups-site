## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Edison

We start by identifying the water source and shutting it off at the fixture or main if it's still active, then move to extraction with truck-mounted pumps for anything beyond an inch or two of standing water. In homes with finished basements, that means pulling baseboard and cutting drywall at flood-cut height so we can get airflow behind the wall cavity, because trapped moisture there is what turns a one-day water event into a two-week mold problem. For split-levels and capes with below-grade bedrooms, a common layout across North Edison and Stelton, we pay close attention to closet and subfloor cavities where water tracks farther than it looks. Once materials are out and surfaces are clean, we set drying equipment calibrated to the room's moisture readings rather than a fixed schedule, and we verify dryness with a moisture meter before closing anything back up.
