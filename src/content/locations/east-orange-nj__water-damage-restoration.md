## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in East Orange

The first call is almost always about stopping the source and figuring out how many units are affected, since a riser failure in a multi-family building rarely stays contained to one apartment. We extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable units, then map moisture in the plaster, brick, and subfloor so drying equipment goes where the water actually traveled, not just where it's visible. Pre-war plaster and brick hold moisture longer than drywall and release it slowly, so we monitor readings over several days rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule. Structural drying is tracked against the IICRC S500 standard, with daily moisture logs so the building owner and any insurance adjuster can see exactly when materials reach dry standard. For basement losses tied to the Second River or a backed-up sewer lateral, we sanitize and deodorize as part of the same visit rather than treating it as a separate step.
