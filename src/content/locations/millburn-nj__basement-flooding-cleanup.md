## Why Millburn Basements Flood

Two things drive most of the calls we get in this town. First, proximity to the Rahway: homes near Taylor Park, the downtown retail corridor on Millburn Avenue, and the lower-lying streets feeding toward the river see water intrude from the outside in, pushing through foundation cracks, window wells, and sump systems that simply can't keep pace. Second, the age of the housing stock itself. Pre-1950 colonials and Tudors in Glenwood and around the Millburn train station often still run cast-iron drain lines and aging clay or copper supply piping below grade. When a line fails or backs up, water comes from inside, and it tends to carry sediment and rust that complicates cleanup. Either way, the finished basements common in Short Hills and Old Short Hills mean the loss usually includes carpet, drywall, built-in cabinetry, and sometimes wine cellars or media rooms, not just a bare slab.

## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Millburn

We start by identifying whether you're dealing with river-driven groundwater intrusion or an internal pipe or sewer failure, because the extraction and drying plan differs. Standing water gets pumped and extracted first, then we assess what's salvageable versus what has to come out: saturated drywall, baseboards, and wet insulation rarely dry in place fast enough to stop secondary damage. Antique woodwork, built-in shelving, and finished walls common in the older Millburn homes get evaluated piece by piece rather than stripped wholesale, since a lot of that millwork predates drywall-era construction and is worth saving if it hasn't fully delaminated. Once materials are pulled, we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's moisture load and monitor drying with moisture meters until readings hit dry standard, following IICRC S500 drying guidelines throughout.

## Millburn Insurance Coordination

Flood versus water damage is the first question every adjuster asks in this town, and it matters because standard homeowners policies typically exclude river and surface-water flooding, which means a basement flooded by Rahway overflow may fall under a separate flood policy while a burst pipe falls under standard water damage coverage. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings that support whichever claim applies, and we've seen enough Millburn claims from both categories, river flooding near downtown and internal pipe failures in the older homes, to know what adjusters want to see from each.

## Local note

Homes close to Taylor Park and the stretch of downtown that flooded during Ida often have sump pumps that were sized for ordinary groundwater, not river overtopping. When the Rahway backs up, those pumps run constantly but can't out-pace the volume coming in through foundation walls, so the basement floods even with the system working correctly. We check pump capacity and discharge routing as part of the initial assessment on any property in that flood-prone band, since it affects how quickly the water recedes once we start extraction.
