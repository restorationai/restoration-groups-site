## Our Mold Remediation Process in Woodbridge

We start with containment, because the goal is to stop spores from migrating into rooms that are still clean. In a split-level with central HVAC, that usually means sealing supply and return vents in the affected zone before any disturbance of moldy material, since forced air can carry spores through an entire house in a single heating cycle. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet padding, are removed and bagged for disposal rather than treated in place, while structural wood and subfloor are cleaned, treated, and dried per the IICRC S500 standard. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run for the duration of the containment period, and we verify moisture content in framing and subfloor with a meter before closing anything back up. On older Woodbridge Proper and Fords homes with plaster rather than drywall, we check lath and furring strips separately, since plaster can look dry on the face while the wood behind it is still holding moisture weeks after the original leak.
