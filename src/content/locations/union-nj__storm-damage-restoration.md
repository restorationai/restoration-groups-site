## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Union

The first visit is about stopping the loss, not documenting it for later. Union's finished and semi-finished basements complicate this step: paneling, carpet over slab, and furred-out stud walls all trap moisture behind finished surfaces, so moisture mapping with a meter matters more here than in a home with an open, unfinished basement. We document every reading and photograph as we go, since that record is what your adjuster will ask for.
