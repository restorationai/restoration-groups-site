## Why Elizabeth Properties See Biohazard Needs

Elizabeth's housing stock skews old and dense. A lot of the two- and three-family buildings in Midtown and North Elizabeth were converted from single-family homes decades ago, which means shared basements, common laundry areas, and plumbing stacks that run through more than one unit. When a biohazard event happens in one apartment, the affected material can travel further than it would in a detached house, through subfloors, wall cavities, or a shared drain line. In Elizabethport and Bayway, low elevation and proximity to the Elizabeth River add another layer: storm surge and tidal flooding events have pushed sewage and contaminated floodwater into basements and ground-floor units, which is its own category of exposure separate from an isolated incident upstairs.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Elizabeth

We start with containment, sealing off the affected area so nothing tracks into hallways, stairwells, or adjoining units, which matters more in multifamily buildings than in a single-family home. Affected porous materials (carpet, subflooring, drywall) are typically removed rather than cleaned, since they can't be fully sanitized. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned and disinfected with EPA-registered products, and the space is tested before we call it clear. For needle or sharps-related cleanup, our crews follow OSHA bloodborne pathogen handling protocols and dispose of regulated waste through licensed medical waste transport, not standard trash pickup. Every step is photographed and logged, which matters later for insurance and, in rental buildings, for the property manager's own records.

## Elizabeth Insurance & HOA Coordination

A large share of Elizabeth's housing is landlord-owned two- and three-family buildings, which means biohazard jobs here often involve a property owner who isn't the person who called us, plus a tenant who needs somewhere else to stay in the meantime. We document scope and condition with enough detail for a landlord's policy and a tenant's renters' policy to both make sense of the claim, and we coordinate scheduling around occupied units so common areas stay usable while the affected space is being handled. For condo and co-op buildings near Midtown, we work directly with building management or superintendents on access and notification, since those buildings often have their own protocols for anything involving shared hallways or elevators.
