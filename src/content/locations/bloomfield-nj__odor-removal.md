## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process

We start by identifying what's actually generating the odor, not just where it's strongest, because in a Bloomfield two-family or a Bloomfield Center Tudor, the smell at the top of the stairs is often coming from saturated material two rooms away. For sewage-related odor from basement backups, we treat the source materials first with enzymatic cleaners, then follow with hydroxyl generators, which work safely in occupied spaces, important in a two-family where one unit may still have tenants living upstairs while the basement is being treated. For smoke odor from a kitchen or electrical fire, we typically pair thermal fogging, which mimics how smoke originally penetrated wood framing and cabinetry, with sealing of affected surfaces so the odor doesn't reassert itself once humidity rises. Ozone treatment has its place, mainly in vacant commercial spaces along Broad Street or in a vacated unit, because it requires the space to be unoccupied for safety reasons, but we don't default to it in a home that's still lived in.
