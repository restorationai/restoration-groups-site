## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Clark

An inspection starts with a visual walkthrough and moisture mapping, checking framing, subfloor, and finished basement walls with a penetrating moisture meter rather than relying on how a surface feels or looks. In homes near Robinson's Branch or Valley Road where basements have a flood history, we pay particular attention to the bottom two feet of drywall and the back side of basement paneling, since water wicks upward into drywall and sits against finished walls long after carpet reads dry. Air sampling follows, comparing spore counts inside the home against an outdoor control sample, with surface swabs or tape lifts taken from any visible growth. Samples go to an independent lab, and the written report documents what was found, where, and at what concentration, which is the paperwork most insurance carriers and homebuyers require before any next step.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

Findings are backed by instruments, not guesswork: moisture meters, hygrometers for relative humidity, thermal imaging to spot temperature differentials that suggest trapped moisture behind finished surfaces, and spore-trap air sampling pumps for the lab analysis. Our team follows industry sampling protocol consistent with the IICRC S520 mold remediation standard, and our firm holds IICRC certification, which shapes how we document chain of custody on samples and how we write the report so it holds up with an adjuster or a buyer's attorney.
