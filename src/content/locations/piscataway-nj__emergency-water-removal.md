## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in Piscataway

The first thing we do on arrival is figure out what kind of water we're dealing with, because that changes everything downstream. A supply-line break in a Stelton split-level is clean water and gets treated differently than river-pushed water near Johnson Park, which carries silt, fuel residue, and bacterial contamination and gets handled as a Category 3 loss from the start. Extraction comes first, pulling standing water with truck-mounted and portable units, then we map moisture in the walls, subfloor, and framing with meters so we know what's actually wet versus what looks wet. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, but the timeline shifts with the building: a finished Society Hill basement with drywall and modern insulation dries faster than an older Possumtown or Arbor cape with plaster or dense-pack cavities that release moisture slowly even after the surface reads dry. Cast-iron drain backups get sanitized as part of the cleanup, not treated as a separate step, since contaminated water from a failed stack behaves more like a sewage event than a simple leak.
