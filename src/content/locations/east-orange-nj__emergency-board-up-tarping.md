## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in East Orange

On arrival, we assess what's actually holding, not just what's visibly broken. On a shared-roof apartment building, that means checking adjoining units and the parapet line before a single tarp goes up, since a patch that looks fine from the street can still be feeding water into a neighbor's ceiling. We board broken or blown-out windows and doors with exterior-grade plywood sized and fastened to the actual opening, not a generic sheet, and we tarp roofs using reinforced poly anchored through battens rather than just nailed at the edges, which matters on East Orange's older roof decking where repeated fastening can weaken the substrate. Everything is documented with photos before and after for the insurance file, and we coordinate with property management on multi-unit buildings so every affected tenant's unit gets checked, not just the one where the damage originated.
