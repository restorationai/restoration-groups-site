## Our Mold Remediation Process in Englewood

We start with containment, not demolition. Negative air pressure gets set up around the affected area using physical barriers so spores don't travel into the rest of the house or, in commercial spaces near Bergen PAC and the surrounding retail corridor, into adjoining units. From there we remove the material that's actually compromised, which in a pre-1950 East Hill home often means hand-removing plaster in sections rather than tearing out full walls, since the lath underneath frequently dries and holds once the moisture source is gone. In newer West Englewood construction with drywall and engineered flooring, removal tends to be faster but requires closer attention to subfloor moisture readings before anything goes back. HEPA air scrubbers run throughout, surfaces get treated and sealed, and we document moisture content at each stage so the file matches what your insurance carrier expects to see.
