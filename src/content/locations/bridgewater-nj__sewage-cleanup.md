## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

We start by containing the affected area and identifying the category of contamination, since raw sewage is always treated as Category 3 water under IICRC guidelines, meaning every porous material it touches, carpet pad, drywall below the flood line, baseboards, insulation, typically has to come out rather than be dried in place.
