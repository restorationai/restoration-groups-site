## Our Mold Remediation Process in Bayonne

We start with containment, because dense, attached housing means airborne spores have a short trip to a shared wall or stairwell. In a Bayonne rowhome or multi-family, that often means sealing off a stairwell or utility chase before any demolition begins, not just the room where growth is visible. Negative air machines and HEPA filtration keep spores from migrating into occupied units above or beside the affected space, which matters in rental buildings where a tenant complaint in one unit can lead to a mold call in the unit next door. Once containment is set, we remove affected porous material, treat structural surfaces per IICRC S520 guidance, and run drying equipment calibrated to the humidity load typical of a below-grade Bayonne basement rather than a generic drying schedule. We document moisture readings throughout so there's a clear record for your insurance carrier or, where applicable, a landlord's file.

## Bayonne Insurance & HOA Coordination

Mold claims in Bayonne frequently trace back to a covered water loss, a failed sump pump, a supply line split behind a washer, an overflow from an upstairs unit, so documenting the original source matters as much as documenting the mold itself. As an IICRC Certified Firm, we photograph and log moisture intrusion points before remediation starts, which gives your adjuster the chain of causation they need to process a claim tied to sudden water damage rather than long-term neglect. For multi-family buildings near Bayonne Crossing or along the Route 440 corridor, we coordinate directly with property managers so the paperwork lines up with what the building's insurance carrier expects from a commercial mold claim.
