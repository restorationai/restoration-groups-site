## Why Edison Properties See Mold Issues

Edison's housing stock is a mix of postwar capes and split-levels built with original cast-iron drains and galvanized supply lines, plus a newer wave of large multifamily complexes around North Edison and the Route 1 corridor. Both patterns create mold risk in different ways. In the older homes, aging drain lines crack or back up slowly, feeding moisture into finished basement framing for months before anyone notices a problem. In the multifamily buildings, shared wall cavities and HVAC returns can carry spores between units faster than a homeowner expects, especially in buildings with limited exterior ventilation. Add Central Jersey's humid summers and you get finished basements that rarely drop below the moisture threshold mold needs to spread, roughly 60 percent relative humidity sustained over 48 hours.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Edison

We start with containment, sealing the affected area with poly sheeting and negative air pressure so spores don't travel into HVAC returns, which matters more in Edison's split-levels where the basement often shares ductwork with the main living floor. From there we remove and bag affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet padding, per IICRC S520 guidelines, and HEPA-vacuum every surface before applying an antimicrobial treatment. For homes near Mill Brook or the lower-lying streets south of Route 27, we also check subfloor and sill plate moisture with a meter before closing out, since surface-dry framing in these areas can still be wet two inches in. Work typically finishes with a post-remediation verification, either a visual and moisture check or a third-party clearance test depending on the scope and what the property or insurer requires.

## Edison Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowners policies exclude mold that results from "long-term" moisture or flood water, which creates friction when a basement near the Raritan floods and then grows mold weeks later, since the carrier may try to separate the flood claim from the mold claim. We document moisture readings, photos, and a timeline at intake specifically so that distinction holds up if a carrier pushes back. For condo and townhome associations in North Edison and around Oak Tree, we've also seen boards require a written remediation protocol and sometimes a third-party clearance report before owners can move back in or before common-area work gets approved, and we build that documentation into the job from day one rather than adding it after the fact.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold

Containment barriers, HEPA air scrubbers, and negative air machines are standard on every job, sized to the square footage of the affected area rather than a flat setup. We use moisture meters and sometimes a thermal camera to trace how far contamination has spread behind finished walls, which is especially useful in the capes and split-levels where water travels along sill plates and framing long before it shows on paint or drywall. Antimicrobial application and HEPA vacuuming follow material removal, and we coordinate any third-party air clearance testing the property or its insurer requires before we consider the job closed.
