Avenel sits within Woodbridge Township in Middlesex County, an area built out heavily in the postwar decades with slab-on-grade and low-crawl-space homes. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Avenel

The first call is about stopping the source and assessing what's wet, not just what's visible. On a slab home, water under vinyl or laminate flooring can travel yards from the original leak point before anyone notices a soft spot, so we use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the spread before pulling a single board. Extraction comes next, using truck-mounted or portable units depending on access, followed by placement of air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the room's cubic footage, not a one-size-fits-all setup. In homes with the original plaster-and-lath still intact in a few rooms (common in Avenel's older pockets near the rail corridor), drying takes longer than drywall because plaster holds moisture deeper in the wall assembly, and we adjust the equipment schedule accordingly rather than pulling out early and risking hidden mold growth.
