## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Livingston

We start by pulling standing water with truck-mounted extraction, then check what's underneath. A finished basement in Livingston usually means carpet over pad, sometimes over a vapor barrier, sometimes not, and that layering changes how fast moisture travels sideways into wall cavities. We pull baseboards and drill small weep holes in drywall where needed so trapped water has somewhere to go, then set air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the square footage and the amount of saturated material, not a generic room count. Drying is tracked daily with moisture meters against the framing and subfloor, following the IICRC S500 standard for water damage, until readings return to dry baseline rather than just "looks dry." For homes near Northland and Collins where sump pumps are common, we also check the discharge line and pit before we leave, since a flooded basement with a failed pump will flood again on the next storm if the root cause isn't addressed.
