**Sewage backup in your Newark basement or first floor?** Raw sewage from a backed-up sewer line or overwhelmed combined sewer isn't a wait-and-see problem, and in a city where storm surges push water back through floor drains and toilets within minutes, every hour of contact with contaminated water raises the risk to flooring, drywall, and anyone in the building. We answer 24/7 and bring IICRC-trained technicians who treat sewage cleanup and sanitization as its own category of work, not an extension of routine water extraction.

## Why Newark Properties See Sewage Backups

Newark runs largely on a combined sewer system, meaning stormwater and sanitary waste share the same pipes through most of the city. When heavy rain overwhelms that system, the first place it looks for relief is the lowest point it can reach, which is often a basement floor drain or a ground-floor toilet in the Ironbound or Downtown Newark. The Ironbound's low elevation along the Passaic River made this painfully clear during Hurricane Ida, when backups hit basements that had never flooded before. Add in the city's older clay and cast-iron lateral lines, many original to pre-war brick multifamilies and frame two- and three-families, and you have pipes that are more prone to root intrusion, bellies, and collapse than anything built in the last few decades. A cracked lateral under a Weequahic or Vailsburg two-family can quietly back up a building for weeks before anyone notices the smell.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Newark

We start by identifying the category of contamination, since a sewer line backup is treated as Category 3 water regardless of how it reaches a structure, and that classification drives everything that follows. Technicians extract standing waste water, remove and discard porous materials that contacted contamination (carpet, pad, baseboards, and often lower drywall), then clean and disinfect hard surfaces with EPA-registered antimicrobials. In the older multifamily buildings common from Forest Hill to University Heights, we pay close attention to where flooring meets original plaster walls and where plumbing risers run between units, because a failure on one floor routinely tracks down through two more. Air movers and negative air setups run until moisture readings and odor confirm the space is dry, not just surface-dry.

## Newark Insurance & HOA Coordination

Sewer backup coverage is often a separate rider on a homeowner's or landlord's policy, and adjusters want documentation that shows the source was a line failure or municipal system surcharge rather than deferred maintenance. We photograph the affected areas, log moisture readings, and provide the kind of itemized scope that carriers expect before approving mitigation costs, which matters for the landlords and property managers common across Newark's rental stock near the arena district and university corridor. For multifamily owners managing several units at once, we coordinate scheduling so tenants aren't displaced longer than necessary.
