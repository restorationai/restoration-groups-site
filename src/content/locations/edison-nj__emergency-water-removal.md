## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in Edison

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted units before it has time to wick further into drywall and subfloor. In Edison's finished basements, that often means pulling baseboard and checking wall cavities early, since water trapped behind paneling or drop ceilings in a Stelton or Nixon rec room doesn't show on the surface the way it does on bare concrete. Moisture readings get taken on framing, subfloor, and any slab-on-grade sections, because a basement that reads dry at the carpet can still be holding water in the sill plate. From there we set air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the space, not a generic room count, and we document every reading for the insurance file as we go. Cast-iron drain failures and older copper supply lines get flagged separately, since a leak that keeps recurring from a failing pipe isn't something extraction alone solves.
