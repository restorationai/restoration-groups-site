## Why North Bergen Properties See Mold Issues

North Bergen is one of the densest towns in the state, and a lot of that density is early 20th-century multi-family walkups with original cast-iron drain lines. Those pipes corrode from the inside, and a slow leak behind a wall near Bergenline or along the Fairview border can feed mold growth for months before a tenant or landlord notices the smell. The 2021 Ida flooding pushed storm drains past capacity across the western flats, and basements that took in water then are still the ones we hear about when summer humidity spikes again. Add overcrowded rental units with limited ventilation, and bathrooms and kitchens in older buildings become steady mold sources even without a single flood event.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in North Bergen

The approach changes depending on what's behind the wall. In the pre-war walkups common around the Bergenline business district, we're often dealing with plaster and lath instead of drywall, which means containment has to account for dust and debris that modern materials don't produce, plus a slower drying curve once the source is fixed. We isolate the affected area with physical containment and negative air pressure, remove the mold-affected material, treat the structural surfaces, and verify the space is dry before closing anything back up. In hillside two- and three-family homes near Woodcliff, crawl spaces and sloped foundations mean we're also checking for moisture migration that doesn't show up on the surface layer, which is why we open up more than just the visibly stained section.

## North Bergen Insurance & HOA Coordination

Much of North Bergen's housing stock is landlord-owned multi-family rental property rather than HOA-governed condos, so the coordination problem is usually tenant access and landlord notification rather than association sign-off. We document the affected units with photos and moisture readings so the paperwork holds up whether the claim goes through a property owner's policy or a tenant's renter's insurance. For the commercial spaces along the Tonnelle Avenue and Bergenline corridors, that documentation also needs to satisfy a business's property manager or a retail tenant's lease requirements before work can close out.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold

Containment barriers and negative air machines keep spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal, which matters more in attached multi-family buildings where airflow between units is harder to control. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run throughout the job, and we use moisture meters to confirm that wall cavities and subfloor are actually dry, not just dry to the touch, before we consider the area cleared. Our team works under IICRC Certified Firm credentials, so the drying and remediation steps follow the same structural drying standard regardless of whether the job is a hillside single-family or a Bergenline storefront.
