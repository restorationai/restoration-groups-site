## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Elizabeth

We start with a walkthrough that maps where smoke actually traveled, not just where the fire was. In a Westminster three-family or a Midtown rowhouse, that usually means checking units that weren't involved in the fire at all, since soot migrates through shared chases and ceiling cavities. From there, we separate dry-sponge cleaning for light soot on hard surfaces from wet cleaning and deodorization for protein residue and heavier char, and we test odor penetration into porous materials like plaster, baseboards, and cabinetry before deciding what can be cleaned versus what needs to come out. Contents, especially in multi-unit buildings where tenants have belongings packed tight, often get pulled for off-site cleaning and short-term storage so the unit can be fully sealed and treated. Air scrubbing and thermal fogging follow once surfaces are clean, since odor molecules trapped in old plaster don't respond to surface wiping alone.
