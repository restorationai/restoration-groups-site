## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in New Brunswick

The first call is about stopping the source and protecting what's salvageable, not paperwork. Once we're on site, we extract standing water with truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how the building is accessed (a walk-up near Rutgers Village needs different equipment staging than a downtown high-rise with loading-dock access), then move to moisture mapping with meters to find out how far the water traveled under flooring and inside wall cavities. In the Fifth Ward and Highland Park border blocks, that often means pulling baseboards on plaster walls, since plaster holds moisture longer than drywall and will read dry at the surface while still saturated underneath. Drying equipment stays in place and gets monitored until readings confirm the structure is back to a normal moisture range, which we document for your insurance file.
