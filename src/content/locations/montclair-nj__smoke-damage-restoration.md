## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Montclair

We start with a walk-through to map how far smoke traveled, which in a three-story Victorian with open stairwells usually means every floor, not just the room of origin. Porous materials like plaster, upholstery, and old wood flooring get tested before we decide what can be cleaned versus what has to come out. Dry soot on plaster is vacuumed with HEPA equipment before any wet cleaning, because adding moisture to dry soot on a lime-plaster wall can smear residue permanently into the surface. Hard surfaces and sealed wood trim get wiped down with the appropriate cleaning agent for the soot type (protein, synthetic, or oil-based), and we run thermal foggers or hydroxyl generators to break down odor molecules that have settled into wall cavities, not just mask them. HVAC ducts get inspected and cleaned when smoke has circulated through forced-air systems, which is common in homes with finished basements that share return lines with the main floors.
