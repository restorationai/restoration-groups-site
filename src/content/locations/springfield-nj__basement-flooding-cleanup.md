## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process

We start by pulling standing water with truck-mounted extraction, then map moisture behind walls and under flooring, because a basement that looks dry at the baseboard can still be saturated in the stud bay or under a 1940s hardwood subfloor. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the square footage and the material, not a one-size-fits-all setup. In older capes with masonry or stone foundations we pay particular attention to the foundation wall itself, since porous block can wick moisture for longer than drywall or framed walls would suggest on a moisture meter. Where sump pumps or French drains failed during the flood, we document that failure point for the insurance file and flag it so a licensed trade can address it before the next storm.
