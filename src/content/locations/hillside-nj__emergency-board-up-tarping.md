## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Hillside

We start by checking what's actually structurally sound before anything gets covered: a scorched header or a roof deck softened by standing water changes how and where we anchor. Plywood goes up with construction screws instead of nails so panels hold through wind events instead of working loose. On pitched roofs common to the capes and colonials around Conant, we run tarps under the ridge line and batten them down with furring strips rather than just staples, because a flapping tarp edge will tear itself open in a nor'easter faster than the hole it's covering.
