## Why Westfield Properties See Mold Issues

Westfield's housing stock works against it in a few specific ways. Clay sewer laterals and knob-and-tube-era plumbing retrofits common in homes near Downtown Westfield and The Gardens tend to hide slow leaks inside wall cavities rather than announcing themselves at the surface, which means moisture can sit against lath and plaster for weeks. The town's proximity to the Rahway River's Robinson's Branch, combined with the heavy old-growth tree canopy around Tamaques Park and Mindowaskin Park, keeps soil moisture and ambient humidity higher than you'd expect this far from the coast, especially around foundations that were never built with modern vapor barriers. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Westfield

We start with containment, because an open floor plan or an unsealed HVAC return in an older Westfield home can spread spores from a basement gym to an upstairs bedroom in a single heating cycle. Negative air pressure and physical barriers go up before any affected material is touched. From there we remove and bag contaminated drywall, insulation, or flooring per IICRC S520 guidelines, HEPA-vacuum the structural framing and remaining surfaces, and apply an antimicrobial treatment before clearance. In homes with original plaster, which absorbs and releases moisture more slowly than modern drywall, we extend drying time and monitor moisture content in the lath itself rather than relying on surface readings alone, since a wall can read dry at the paint while the plaster base coat underneath is still saturated.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold

Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously through containment to manage airborne spore counts, and we track humidity and temperature with data loggers so there's a documented record for your insurance file, not just a verbal assurance that the space is dry. On finished basement jobs, where owners have invested heavily in flooring, trim, and built-ins, we pull baseboards and test behind them before deciding whether a wall section needs to come out at all. That selective approach matters more in Westfield than in newer construction, because matching original trim and plaster finishes afterward is harder and more expensive than in a house built in the last twenty years.
