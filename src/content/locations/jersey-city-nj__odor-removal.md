**Odor that won't quit after a kitchen fire, a sewage backup, or months of damp carpet underlayment is one of the most common calls we get from Jersey City property owners.** Between the combined-sewer backups that hit cellars in the Heights and Bergen-Lafayette and the smoke that travels through shared HVAC risers in Newport and Exchange Place high-rises, odor in this city rarely stays contained to one room. Masking a smell with a spray is a short-term fix. Getting rid of it for good means finding what's actually holding the odor and treating that material directly.

## Why Jersey City Properties See Odor Problems

Jersey City's housing stock splits into two very different odor risks. In the frame rowhouses and brownstones of the Heights, Bergen-Lafayette, and Greenville, cellars built before modern waterproofing take on groundwater and sewer backup during heavy storms, and that moisture sits against old joists and subfloor long enough to produce a musty, sour smell that outlasts the water itself. At Newport and along the Exchange Place waterfront, stacked condo and rental construction means a grease fire two floors up or a smoke event in one unit can pull odor through shared corridors, elevator shafts, and HVAC returns into units that were never touched by flame. Humidity off the Hudson doesn't help either: odor-causing compounds bind more readily to porous surfaces in damp air, which is most of the year here.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process

We start by identifying the source material, not just the smell. Smoke residue, sewage contamination, and pet or mildew odors each call for a different combination of treatment. For surface-level smoke or cooking odors we typically run thermal fogging to replicate how smoke particles originally traveled, reaching into cracks and soft goods a wipe-down would miss. For contaminated cellars and basements common to the Heights and Greenville, we pull affected baseboard, insulation, or subflooring where odor has soaked in, then treat remaining structure with hydroxyl generators, which work without the off-gassing restrictions ozone requires. Ozone treatment stays in our toolkit for unoccupied units and vacant properties between tenants, since it's effective but requires the space to be cleared during treatment and ventilated after. We verify results with a walk-through before calling a job closed, not just a timer going off on the equipment.

## Jersey City Insurance & HOA Coordination

Condo and co-op buildings near Newport and Exchange Place almost always route odor and smoke claims through the association's master policy as well as the individual unit owner's coverage, and board managers want documentation that shows the treatment addressed the source, not just the smell. We photograph affected materials before removal and provide a treatment summary that building management can hand to the association's insurer. For rowhouse and multifamily owners in Downtown Jersey City, Journal Square, and Greenville, we document per-unit findings separately when a landlord needs to show an adjuster that each affected unit was treated independently rather than lumped into one claim.
