## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in West Orange

Category 3 water, the technical term for sewage-contaminated water, carries bacteria and pathogens that require different handling than a clean water loss. We start by identifying and removing standing sewage with truck-mounted extraction, then isolate the affected zone with containment barriers so contamination doesn't spread into unaffected rooms. Porous materials that have been in contact with raw sewage, drywall below the flood line, carpet padding, baseboards, typically can't be sanitized and have to come out. Everything that stays gets cleaned and treated with EPA-registered antimicrobials, and we dry the structure per the IICRC S500 standard, monitoring moisture in framing and subfloor until readings return to normal. In finished basements, which make up most of the postwar splits and ranches around Pleasantdale, that often means pulling baseboard and a section of lower drywall to dry the stud cavity properly rather than just surface-drying what's visible.
