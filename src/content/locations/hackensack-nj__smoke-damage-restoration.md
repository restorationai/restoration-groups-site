## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Hackensack

We start by identifying the smoke type, because dry smoke from a fast, high-heat fire behaves differently than the thick, oily residue left by a smoldering fire in an insulated modern unit. Dry soot wipes and vacuums off plaster and trim with less smearing; wet smoke residue from synthetic furnishings and modern building materials requires solvent-based cleaning agents and more aggressive degreasing before deodorization even starts. From there we HEPA-vacuum all affected surfaces, clean or discard porous materials that can't be fully deodorized, and use thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment depending on how enclosed the space is, since ozone generators aren't appropriate for occupied adjacent units in a shared-hallway building. Hard surfaces, cabinetry, and structural wood get sealed only after odor testing confirms the source is gone, not just masked. Our teams work under IICRC Certified Firm #210213 standards for the cleaning and deodorization protocols we follow on every loss.
