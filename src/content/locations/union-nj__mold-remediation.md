## Our Mold Remediation Process in Union

We start with containment, because Union's finished basements usually share HVAC returns with the living space above, and an open duct run is the fastest way to spread spores to the second floor. Negative air machines and physical barriers go up before any demolition starts. From there we remove affected drywall, paneling, and insulation back to clean, dry material, HEPA-vacuum the framing, and treat remaining structure with an antimicrobial before drying equipment goes in. For the split-levels and colonials common in Washington and Larchmont, that often means working around finished ceilings in the basement-level family room, so we open strategic sections rather than pulling full ceilings whenever the moisture reading allows it. Drying and remediation follow IICRC S520 guidance, and we don't close out a job until post-remediation moisture readings confirm the structure is back to normal range for the material.

## Insurance and Documentation for Union Mold Claims

Mold claims in New Jersey are scrutinized more closely than water claims generally, and carriers want to see a clear link between a covered water event and the mold that followed. We document moisture readings, photograph the extent of growth before any demolition, and keep a material log so the claim ties the mold back to the originating leak or flood rather than looking like a maintenance issue.
