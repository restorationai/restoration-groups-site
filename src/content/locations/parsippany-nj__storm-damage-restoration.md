## Why Parsippany Properties See Storm Damage

Parsippany-Troy Hills sits low where the Whippany River, Rockaway River, and Troy Brook converge, and that geography shapes how storms hit the township differently block by block. Lake Hiawatha took on serious water during Ida in 2021, and residents who were here for Floyd and Irene will tell you the pattern repeats: the river rises faster than the storm drains can move it, and capes and ranches built on slab or shallow crawl spaces take water through foundation walls and window wells before anyone expects it. Up in Mount Tabor, the dense Victorian cottages sit close together under mature tree canopy, so wind events bring down limbs onto porches, gutters, and knob-and-tube-era roof lines that weren't built for modern storm loads. Meanwhile the office parks and hotels along the Waterview commercial district and Route 46 see a different failure mode entirely: flat roofs pond and split, and a hard summer storm can send water through a membrane seam straight into a server room or lobby overnight when nobody's there to catch it early.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Parsippany

We start with a roof and exterior assessment to find where wind or water actually breached the structure, because storm damage often enters in one spot and travels through wall cavities or ceiling assemblies before it shows up as a stain somewhere else. If the roof or windows are compromised, we tarp and board immediately to stop ongoing intrusion, then move to extraction in any flooded basement or lower level, which is common in the lake tracts and anywhere near the Whippany River's floodplain. From there we document every affected material with photos and moisture readings for the insurance file, set drying equipment calibrated to the specific materials involved (plaster and lath in Mount Tabor's older cottages dry slower than drywall in a 1970s split-level), and monitor until readings confirm the structure is back to normal. Downed trees and large debris get coordinated separately from the drying work so neither process holds up the other.
