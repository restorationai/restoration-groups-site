## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Rahway

We start by killing power to affected circuits before anyone steps into standing water, then move to extraction with truck-mounted pumps that can keep pace with a basement that's still taking on water from an active storm. From there we pull out anything holding moisture that can't be salvaged, wet carpet pad, saturated paneling, drywall above the flood line, and we moisture-map the foundation walls with penetrating meters so we know exactly how far the water traveled into masonry and framing. Because so many Rahway basements run on original cast-iron stacks, a backed-up drain is often part of the cause, not just the symptom, so we'll flag a line for camera inspection before we close anything back up. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized for below-grade concrete, which holds moisture far longer than above-grade framing and needs more time and airflow to read dry all the way through.
