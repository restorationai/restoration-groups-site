## Our Mold Remediation Process in Montclair

We start with a visual inspection and moisture mapping to find out how far the problem has spread past what is visible, which matters more in plaster-and-lath homes than in newer drywall construction because moisture travels differently through old horsehair plaster. Once we know the scope, we set up containment with poly sheeting and negative air pressure to keep spores from migrating into the rest of the house while work is underway. Affected porous materials come out, surfaces are treated with an antimicrobial, and drying follows the IICRC S520 mold remediation standard rather than guesswork on when a space is actually dry. Older plaster releases moisture more slowly than drywall, so we extend drying time in those rooms instead of closing out early on a schedule that works for newer construction.
