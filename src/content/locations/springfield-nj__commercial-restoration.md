## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Springfield

We start with a walkthrough that documents every affected area for the property manager, the business owner, and the insurance carrier at the same time, because commercial claims usually involve more than one party signing off. Extraction and structural drying come next, using the same moisture-mapping approach we'd use on a residence but scaled to the square footage, open floor plans, and drop-ceiling systems common in Route 22 corridor retail spaces. Where fire or smoke is involved, we isolate HVAC zones before cleaning so soot doesn't recirculate into unaffected suites, which matters most in the strip centers where several businesses share one rooftop unit. Throughout the job, we coordinate scheduling around the business's operating hours when the space can stay partially open, and work straight through when it can't, because a vacant storefront on a commercial strip is a visible problem for the whole center, not just the one tenant.
