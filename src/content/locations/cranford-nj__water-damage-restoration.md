## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Cranford

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out of basements and crawl spaces before it has a chance to sit against old mortar joints or rotted sill plates. From there we moisture-map the structure, because in a balloon-framed Cranford colonial, water travels inside wall cavities far beyond where it entered, and a wall that reads dry at the surface can still be saturated behind the plaster lath. Drying is calibrated to that reality: we dry structures per the IICRC S500 standard, which means monitoring moisture content daily rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule. Sunny Acres homes with newer additions tacked onto older cores often need two different drying plans for the same structure, one for the original plaster section and one for the drywall addition.
