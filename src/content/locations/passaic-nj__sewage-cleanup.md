**Raw sewage backing up through a floor drain in Downtown Passaic?** Call now, we answer 24/7 and dispatch a crew for sewage cleanup and sanitization before contamination spreads into subflooring and wall cavities.

Passaic's combination of an aging sewer network and a river that rises fast makes sewage backups a recurring call for us, not a rare one. The city sits low against the Passaic River, and blocks near the water took on serious flooding during both Irene in 2011 and Ida in 2021. When the river or storm drains surcharge, municipal sewer lines back up into the lowest fixtures in a building, usually a basement floor drain or a first-floor toilet, and that water is category 3 black water from the moment it appears. It carries bacteria, pathogens and fine solids that no homeowner should try to mop up with a shop vac.

## Why Passaic Properties See Sewage Backups

Much of Passaic's housing stock, especially the brick multi-family walkups and two- and three-family frame houses in the Third Ward and Fourth Ward, was built with cast-iron drain lines that are now close to a century old. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out, and in a dense rental city where multiple units share one stack, a partial blockage in one apartment can push sewage back into a neighbor's unit below. Add in flat or minimal-pitch lot grading near the river and heavy rain overwhelming the storm and sanitary systems at once, and you get backups that show up with little warning, often in unfinished or unventilated basements where the smell and the standing water go unnoticed until it has already soaked into framing.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Passaic

We start by containing the affected area and extracting standing sewage with truck-mounted or portable pumps, keeping contaminated water from migrating into unaffected rooms or stairwells, which matters in the tighter floor plans common in Passaic's older multi-family buildings. Porous materials that have absorbed black water, carpet padding, baseboards, sometimes drywall, generally can't be sanitized and get removed rather than dried in place. Remaining structural materials are cleaned, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and dried following the IICRC S500 standard for water damage. We document every affected surface with photos before demolition starts, since that record is what supports an insurance claim later.

## Passaic Insurance Coordination

Sewer backup is typically handled as a separate endorsement from standard water damage on a homeowner's or landlord's policy, and plenty of Passaic property owners don't realize they have it, or don't, until a claim is filed. We photograph and itemize the loss as we work so your adjuster has what they need, and because we carry NJ Home Improvement Contractor licensing through the Division of Consumer Affairs, we're set up to work directly with carriers on both owner-occupied homes and the multi-unit rental properties common throughout the Third Ward and near the Main Avenue business district.
