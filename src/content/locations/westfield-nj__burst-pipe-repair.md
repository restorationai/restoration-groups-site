## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Westfield

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Burst Pipe

Water from a pressurized supply line behaves differently than a slow roof leak, it moves fast and finds every gap in subfloor and wall cavity before you've finished finding the shutoff valve.
