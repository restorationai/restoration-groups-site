## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Paramus

Sewage cleanup isn't water extraction with an extra step. Category 3 water, which is what raw sewage is classified as, carries bacteria and pathogens that require different handling at every stage. We start by identifying the contamination boundary, removing standing water and visibly affected materials (carpet, pad, and in most cases drywall and baseboard that have wicked contact), and bagging anything porous for disposal rather than attempting to dry and save it. Hard surfaces and framing get cleaned, then treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, then re-tested before we move to structural drying. In a finished Paramus basement, that often means pulling back paneling or drop ceilings installed decades after the original build, since those additions can hide moisture pockets long after the surface looks dry. We document every step with photos for the insurance file, since sewage losses almost always involve a claim.
