## Why Somerville Properties See Mold Issues

Somerville's housing stock skews old: early-1900s frame two-families, Victorians, and colonials around Downtown, West End, and Gaston Avenue, many with galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drains that were never built for the finished basements owners have added since. Those basements sit low relative to the Raritan River and Peters Brook, and when the borough floods, as it did during Ida in 2021 and in earlier storms like Floyd and Irene, the water finds its way into sill plates, subfloor, and wall cavities that don't dry on their own. Add the newer mid-rise residential buildings going up near the Main Street business district, where shared plumbing chases and tighter unit spacing mean one slow leak can affect multiple walls before anyone notices, and you have a borough with two very different but equally common mold triggers: old infrastructure and new density.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Somerville

We start with a visual and moisture-mapping walkthrough, checking sill plates, baseboards, and any finished basement framing for elevated moisture content, since in older Somerville homes the drywall or paneling can look dry while the stud behind it is still wet from a flood months earlier. If we find conditions that warrant it, we collect air samples and, where surfaces show visible growth or odor without visible growth, surface tape or swab samples, then send them to an independent, accredited lab rather than reading results ourselves, which keeps the assessment objective for insurance or real estate purposes. Turnaround on lab results typically takes a few business days, and we walk you through the report in plain language, not just spore counts, so you know whether you're looking at a cleaning job or something that needs a remediation plan.

## Somerville Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Somerville often trace back to a covered water event, a burst galvanized line, a sump pump failure, or storm-related flooding near the river, and carriers generally want documentation that ties the mold to that specific loss rather than long-term humidity. We photograph and document moisture readings at the time of inspection so there's a clear record connecting the water intrusion to what we find weeks or months later. For condo and apartment owners in the newer downtown buildings, HOA or property management may also require their own documentation before approving remediation access to shared walls or chases, and we coordinate scheduling around that when it applies.
