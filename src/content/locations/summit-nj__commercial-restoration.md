## Local note

Office and retail buildings in the Brayton School area and along the older stretches of downtown Summit often have finished basements that were converted decades ago into storage or additional office space, with plaster ceilings below grade rather than drywall. Plaster holds moisture longer than modern materials, which means a space can look surface-dry within a day but still be releasing moisture into the structure a week later. We factor that lag into drying timelines on these buildings instead of pulling equipment on a standard schedule built for newer construction.
