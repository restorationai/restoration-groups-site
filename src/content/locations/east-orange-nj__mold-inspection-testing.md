## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in East Orange

We start with a visual and moisture-mapping walkthrough, checking exterior wall cavities, basement sill plates, and any area with a history of plumbing leaks or roof penetration. In multi-family buildings common around the Central Avenue business district, that means checking shared walls and utility chases, since mold spreads differently in connected units than in a standalone two-family home. Where we suspect hidden growth, we use a moisture meter and, when conditions warrant it, a borescope to look inside wall cavities without cutting large openings. Air and surface sampling follow, with samples sent to a third-party lab for species identification and spore counts, compared against outdoor baseline readings taken the same day. We provide written results with a clear explanation of what the numbers mean for your specific property, not just a lab report you have to interpret yourself.
