## Montclair Insurance Considerations

Most homeowners insurance treats a burst pipe as sudden and accidental water discharge, which is typically covered, but carriers want clear documentation that the damage came from a sudden failure and not years of slow, ignored leaking. That distinction matters more in Montclair's older homes, where a galvanized line that's been seeping for a while can complicate a claim if it isn't photographed and explained properly. We provide moisture readings, photos of the affected cavity, and a written scope before any demolition goes further than it has to, which gives your adjuster what they need without a second visit.
