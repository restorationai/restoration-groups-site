## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Edison

We start with extraction, pulling standing water off floors and out of carpet pad before it migrates into subfloor or wicks up drywall. On older Edison homes with plaster or full brick foundations, we check moisture behind the wall assembly, not just the surface reading, because masonry and plaster hold water longer than modern materials show. Drying equipment, air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the room, gets placed and logged, and we track moisture daily against the IICRC S500 standard until materials read dry rather than guessing at a calendar date. For finished basements, that often means pulling baseboard and a strip of drywall to confirm the stud bay behind it is actually dry, not just the painted surface.
