## Our Mold Remediation Process in Paramus

We start with containment, sealing the affected area with poly sheeting and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into the HVAC system or adjacent rooms, which matters in finished basements where ductwork often runs through the same joist bays as the mold-affected framing. From there we remove and bag contaminated porous materials (drywall, insulation, carpet pad) according to IICRC-informed protocols, HEPA-vacuum the remaining structure, and apply an antimicrobial treatment before running air scrubbers to bring airborne spore counts down. In older Paramus homes with cast-iron drain stacks, we also check for the slow leaks around those joints that often turn out to be the real source, since surface mold without an identified moisture source tends to come back within months. Commercial jobs near the mall corridor usually add a step: coordinating after-hours access with property management so retail operations aren't disrupted during the work.
