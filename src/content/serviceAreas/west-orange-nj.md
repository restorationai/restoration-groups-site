## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in West Orange

West Orange's building stock spans grand early-20th-century Victorians in Llewellyn Park, 1920s Tudors and colonials on the slopes below, and postwar splits and ranches in flatter sections like Pleasantdale, most sitting over finished basements rather than crawlspaces or slabs.
