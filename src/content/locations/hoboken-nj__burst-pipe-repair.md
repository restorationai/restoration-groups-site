## Why Hoboken Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

Much of Hoboken's housing stock, from the brick rowhomes of Downtown Hoboken to the converted multi-family buildings near Castle Point, was plumbed decades before PEX and modern copper became standard. Galvanized supply lines and shared stacks running through party walls are common, and when one section fails under winter freeze-thaw cycling or age-related corrosion, the break often sits inside a wall cavity shared by two or three units rather than a single one. Buildings near the Washington Street business district and around Stevens Institute add another layer: ground-floor commercial space, converted student housing, and mixed-use plumbing runs that weren't designed with today's occupancy loads in mind. Because Hoboken sits low relative to the Hudson, with a combined sewer system that's already strained during heavy rain, a burst supply line can combine with backed-up drainage faster here than in towns with more elevation and separated storm sewers.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Hoboken

The first call is almost always about the active leak, so our crew starts by locating the shutoff, confirming the water source, and extracting standing water from flooring and below-grade spaces before it migrates into a neighboring unit. In rowhomes and brownstones with plaster walls, we open only what's necessary to access the broken section, since plaster and lath hold moisture longer than drywall and need targeted drying rather than blanket demolition. In Waterfront and Uptown high-rises, we coordinate with building engineers on riser shutoffs, since a single break can affect units on multiple floors below. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and meters tells us where water actually traveled, which in party-wall construction is often further than it looks from the visible damage. Drying equipment stays in place and is monitored until materials read dry per the IICRC S500 standard, not just until the surface looks finished.

## Hoboken Insurance & HOA Coordination

A large share of Hoboken's housing is condo or co-op, especially in buildings converted from older rowhomes and in newer construction near the waterfront. That means a burst pipe claim frequently involves both the unit owner's policy and the building's master policy, and HOA or condo boards often want documentation before any repair work starts, not after. We photograph and document the break, the affected materials, and the water's path through shared walls, and we provide that record in a format building management and both carriers can use. For ground-floor and below-grade units, especially around Southwest Hoboken where garden-level spaces are common, we also note whether the loss shows any signs of sewer backup contribution, since that can shift which policy responds.
