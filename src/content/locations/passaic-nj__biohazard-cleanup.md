## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Passaic

Every job starts with containment: we isolate the affected area, establish a controlled entry and exit path, and suit the crew in appropriate PPE before any material is touched. Blood, bodily fluids, and sharps are removed and packaged according to OSHA bloodborne pathogen handling practices and transported by a licensed medical waste carrier, not placed in standard trash.
