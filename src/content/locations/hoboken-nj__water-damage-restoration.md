**Water damage emergency in Hoboken?** We answer 24/7, and if you're pulling up soaked carpet in a Downtown Hoboken garden unit or watching a ceiling stain spread in a Midtown walk-up, the clock matters more than the paperwork. Hoboken sits on reclaimed tidal marsh along the Hudson, which means water that gets into a building here rarely stays put, it moves through party walls, settles under subflooring, and finds every low point in a below-grade unit before anyone notices a problem.

## Why Hoboken Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Hoboken's housing stock is 19th-century brownstones and brick rowhomes built before modern waterproofing standards, many with below-grade apartments that sit at or near the water table. When Superstorm Sandy hit in 2012, large sections of the city went under; more recently, Ida's rainfall in 2021 overwhelmed the combined sewer system and pushed water back into basements and garden-level units from Southwest Hoboken to the Waterfront. Add in aging shared plumbing stacks common to party-wall construction, where a failure in one unit's line can saturate the apartment below or next door, and you get a city where water damage is rarely an isolated event. Newer high-rise construction along the Waterfront brings its own risk profile, with sprinkler lines, rooftop mechanicals, and stacked plumbing runs that can send a leak several floors down before it's caught.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Hoboken

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out of basements and garden units with truck-mounted and portable equipment, because floor joists and subfloor in older brownstones absorb moisture fast once water sits for more than a few hours. From there we moisture-map the affected areas, including party walls shared with neighboring units, since water doesn't respect a property line once it's inside lath and plaster. Structural drying follows, calibrated to the building: older plaster and horsehair lath in a Castle Point rowhome releases moisture more slowly than drywall, so drying timelines in a pre-war building often run longer than in a newer Waterfront condo, even when the water volume was the same. We document moisture readings throughout so the drying log holds up if your insurer asks for proof the structure was dried to standard.

## Hoboken Insurance & HOA Coordination

A lot of Hoboken housing is condo or co-op, which means a loss in one unit often involves the building's HOA or management company before a claim can move forward. We're used to coordinating with both the unit owner's carrier and a building's master policy, documenting origin and scope so liability between unit and common area isn't left to guesswork. For multi-unit brownstones converted to condos, we also flag early whether a leak originated above or below grade, since that often determines which policy responds first.

## Local note

Garden and basement units near the Washington Street business district and around Stevens Institute flood differently than units a few blocks inland, largely because Hoboken's storm drains and combined sewer lines back up fastest in the lowest-lying blocks closest to the Hudson. On jobs in those areas we check the sewer cleanout and basement floor drain before we start drying, because a backed-up line will undo extraction work within hours if it isn't addressed first.
