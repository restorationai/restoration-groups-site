## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Jersey City

The first visit is about stopping loss, not paperwork. Crews shut off the source where it's safe to do so, extract standing water with truck-mount and portable units, and pull up flooring or baseboards where water has gotten behind the surface. In brownstones near Journal Square and the Heights, that often means checking plaster walls separately from the drywall in later additions, since plaster holds moisture longer and reads deceptively dry on a surface scan. In Newport and Exchange Place condos, we document moisture readings unit by unit, since a single pipe failure in a stacked building can affect multiple owners and each one may need separate drying logs for their association's insurer. Once materials are extracted, we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's actual moisture load, not a flat formula, and we track drying progress daily against the IICRC S500 standard until materials hit a dry standard, not just a dry feel.
