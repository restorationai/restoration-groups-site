## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Garfield

Floodwater from the Passaic or from a backed-up municipal line is treated as contaminated water from the start, which changes how we approach extraction. Crews pull standing water with truck-mounted pumps, then assess what's underneath: Garfield's older two- and three-family homes often hide plaster-and-lath walls or original hardwood over rough subfloor, both of which hold moisture longer than modern drywall and engineered flooring. We map moisture through walls and framing with meters rather than guessing from surface appearance, apply antimicrobial treatment to flood-contacted materials, and set drying equipment calibrated to the specific assembly, not a generic basement setup. Work follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage drying, and we document every step with photos and moisture readings for your insurance file.
