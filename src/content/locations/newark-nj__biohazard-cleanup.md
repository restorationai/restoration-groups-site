## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Newark

We start with an assessment that identifies every surface and material the incident reached, because porous materials like subfloor, drywall, and older plaster often need to be removed rather than cleaned. Technicians work in full PPE, establish containment to protect the rest of the unit or building, and use EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate to the material and the type of contamination. Affected materials are bagged, labeled, and transported as regulated waste through a licensed disposal path, not placed in a standard dumpster. Once the site is cleaned and disinfected, we document everything with photos and a written scope, which becomes the record a landlord, estate, or insurance carrier needs to close the matter out.
