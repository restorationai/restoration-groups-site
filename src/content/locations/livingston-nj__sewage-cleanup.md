## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Livingston

We start by identifying the contamination category and shutting off the source, whether that's a blocked lateral or a municipal surcharge backing up through the line. Standing sewage and saturated porous materials, carpet pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation, come out first because they can't be sanitized, only removed. Everything that stays gets cleaned and treated with EPA-registered antimicrobials, then the space is dried and monitored per the IICRC S500 standard for water damage.
