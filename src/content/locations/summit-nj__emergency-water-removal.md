## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in Summit

We start with extraction, not demolition. Submersible pumps and truck-mounted extractors pull standing water out of basements and crawl spaces first, because every hour water sits against plaster, baseboard, and original hardwood is an hour closer to permanent loss. From there we moisture-map the affected rooms with penetrating meters and thermal imaging, which matters more in Summit than in newer housing stock because plaster walls and old-growth lumber hold and release moisture on a different schedule than drywall and engineered flooring. We log readings room by room so the drying plan, and the eventual insurance file, reflects what's actually happening inside a wall cavity, not just what's visible. Air movers and dehumidifiers go in next, positioned to protect millwork, wainscoting, and built-ins rather than just hit a drying-time target. Where contents are involved, including furniture, rugs, and family pieces that have been in a Summit house for three or four generations, we document and handle them with the same care the rebuild contractor will expect when finishes need to match.
