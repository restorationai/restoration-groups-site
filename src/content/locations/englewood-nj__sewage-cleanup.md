## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Englewood

We treat every sewage loss as Category 3 water from the first walkthrough, which changes what can be saved. Carpet, carpet pad, and most porous materials that contacted raw sewage get removed rather than cleaned, because they hold contamination deep in the fibers no surface treatment reaches. After extraction, we apply EPA-registered antimicrobials to affected framing and subfloor, set up negative air pressure to keep contaminated air from spreading to unaffected rooms, and dry the structure per the IICRC S500 standard with monitored humidity and moisture readings rather than guessing when it's done. Odor control runs alongside drying, since sewage odor sits in porous materials and doesn't fully clear until the structure itself is dry.
