Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing until pressure finds the weakest joint, usually behind a wall or under a slab where nobody notices until the ceiling below is already stained. Cold snaps make it worse: unheated cellar runs and exterior-wall risers in older Doddtown and Presidential Estates properties are common failure points when overnight temperatures drop fast. ## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in East Orange

The first priority is always stopping the source, whether that means shutting a unit's isolation valve or, in older buildings without one, locating the main. From there we extract standing water, pull baseboards and flooring where moisture has tracked, and use moisture meters to map how far the water traveled behind plaster or into subfloor framing. In multi-unit buildings around Central Avenue and the hospital campus area, that mapping step matters more than usual, because water from a second-floor break can show up as a ceiling stain two units away before it's visible where the pipe actually failed.
