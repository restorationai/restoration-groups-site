## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Englewood

We start by identifying the source and the carrier material, since treating only the air leaves odor to return once humidity rises again. For smoke odor, that often means sealing and treating charred framing or soot-coated drywall before any air treatment begins. We use thermal fogging to replicate how smoke particles originally traveled into cracks and cavities, which reaches pockets a surface wipe-down misses. For organic or sewage-related odors, hydroxyl generators handle occupied spaces safely since they don't require evacuation, while ozone treatment is reserved for unoccupied units or short, controlled sessions where off-gassing materials, pets, and plants are cleared out first. In dense Fourth Ward multifamily buildings, we coordinate timing with building management so hydroxyl units can run without displacing other tenants.
