## Our Emergency Water Removal and Cleanup Process in Plainfield

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mount and portable pumps sized to the volume on-site, not a one-size approach. In pre-war homes near the Van Wyck Brooks Historic District, plaster walls and original wood trim get pulled back from baseboards carefully rather than cut out automatically, since that trim is often original and worth saving if it hasn't delaminated. Moisture mapping comes next: we check subfloor and framing moisture content with meters, not guesswork, because a floor can look dry on top while the joists underneath are still saturated. Drying equipment, air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to room size and material type, stays in place until readings confirm the structure is dry, not just until a set number of days has passed. Everything is documented with photos and moisture logs from the start, since that record is what your carrier will ask for.
