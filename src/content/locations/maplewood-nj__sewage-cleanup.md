## Why Maplewood Properties See Sewage Backups

Maplewood's housing stock, largely built between the 1910s and 1930s, still runs on cast-iron drain lines that were never designed for the volume of modern plumbing fixtures. Add decades of root intrusion and scaling, and those pipes narrow year over year until a heavy rain or a holiday full of houseguests pushes them past capacity. The township's low-lying streets near the East Branch of the Rahway River, which runs through Memorial Park and the Maplewood Village shopping district, compound the problem: when the river overtops during major storms (as it did severely during Ida in 2021), municipal sewer lines can surcharge, sending wastewater back up through basement floor drains and laundry stand pipes before anyone sees standing water outside.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Maplewood

We start by identifying the entry point and shutting off any fixture still feeding the backup, then extract standing sewage with pumps and truck-mounted vacuums rated for contaminated water. In homes with original plaster walls and lath, common in the Hilton and Tuscan sections, we remove affected baseboards and drywall or plaster to the documented waterline rather than guessing at a cut height, since plaster holds moisture and contamination longer than modern drywall and will re-wet a room if left in place. Once gross contamination is removed, we clean and sanitize all affected surfaces, apply antimicrobial treatment, and set containment and air movers to bring the space back to a dry, safe condition, following IICRC S500 drying and S540 biohazard guidelines throughout.
