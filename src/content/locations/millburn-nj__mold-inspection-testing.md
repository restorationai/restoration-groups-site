## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Millburn

We start with a visual walk-through and moisture mapping using a moisture meter and thermal imaging, paying close attention to sill plates, rim joists, and any finished basement walls that back up to grade, since those are where we most often find hidden saturation in this area. If conditions warrant it, we collect air samples and surface swabs for spore testing, comparing indoor spore counts against an outdoor baseline sample taken on the same visit. For older homes with plaster and lath, like many in Millburn Center and Short Hills, we test cavities separately from drywall sections because plaster holds moisture longer and can read dry on the surface while the lath behind it stays wet. Samples go to an independent lab, and we walk homeowners through the results in plain language rather than handing over a report full of genus names with no context.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

Air sampling pumps, spore trap cassettes, and borescopes for looking inside wall cavities without cutting large openings are standard on every inspection. In homes with finished basements or wine cellars, a space Millburn has more of than most towns we serve, we also check HVAC returns and dehumidifier placement, since undersized equipment in a below-grade room is one of the most common reasons mold recurs even after a prior cleanup. Our technicians document everything with photos and moisture readings tied to a floor plan, which becomes useful if the findings end up supporting an insurance claim or a remediation scope of work.
