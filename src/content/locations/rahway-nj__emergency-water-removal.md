Rahway sits low along its namesake river, and the 2021 remnants of Ida put that geography on full display when downtown and the riverfront neighborhoods took on water that reached well above basement window wells in some blocks. Older storms, including the Irene-era flooding years earlier, followed the same pattern: water rises fast along the river corridor and near Rahway River Park, then lingers in low-lying streets long after the rain stops. Housing stock compounds the problem. Victorian and 1920s homes near Downtown Rahway and Central Rahway often sit on cast-iron drain lines and original supply piping that were never sized for modern fixture counts, while postwar capes and colonials further out carry finished basements that trap water against drywall and carpet pad instead of letting it drain.
