## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in North Bergen

We start by identifying the source, because a flooded basement from a clogged storm drain is handled differently than one from a failed sump pump or a cracked foundation wall. Water is extracted first, then we assess what's underneath: many North Bergen basements have been partially finished over the decades, with carpet, paneling, or drop ceilings layered over original brick or parged block foundations. Those layers trap moisture and have to come out if they've been wet more than a few hours. We set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the square footage and material mix, then monitor moisture readings in the framing and subfloor daily until the space reads dry, not just dry to the touch.
