## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in Parsippany

The first move on arrival is stopping the source and pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, because every hour that carpet pad and subfloor stay saturated pushes the job closer to needing material removal instead of drying in place.
