## Why East Brunswick Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Most of East Brunswick's housing stock went up between the 1950s and 1970s, ranches and split-levels with original or early-replacement wiring that wasn't built for today's load of HVAC units, electric ranges and home office equipment. Overloaded circuits and aging panel boxes are a recurring ignition point in these tracts, especially around Old Bridge Turnpike and Tanglewood. Add finished basements, a near-universal feature in this town, and you get a second problem: smoke and heat have nowhere to vent, so soot settles deep into carpet pad, paneling and ductwork instead of dissipating. In the denser condo and townhome clusters near Village of the Golden Triangle, shared walls and attics mean a unit fire can push smoke odor into two or three neighboring units before anyone smells it.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in East Brunswick

We start with board-up and tarping to secure the structure, since a fire-damaged roof or blown-out window left open overnight invites weather damage, pests and theft. From there we separate the job into structural cleaning and contents: charred framing, subfloor and drywall get assessed and cleaned or removed, while salvageable furniture, electronics and textiles move into pack-out and storage so cleaning can happen off-site without holding up the rest of the job. Smoke residue in East Brunswick's older plaster-and-lath ranches behaves differently than in newer drywall construction, it's more porous, so soot migrates further into the wall cavity and takes longer to neutralize. We use thermal fogging and ozone or hydroxyl treatment depending on the material and how deep the odor has traveled, and we document everything with photos and moisture readings for the claim file.

## East Brunswick Insurance & HOA Coordination

Fire losses in East Brunswick's attached and multi-unit properties, particularly around Village of the Golden Triangle and the newer condo developments layered into the older tracts, almost always involve an HOA master policy alongside the homeowner's individual coverage. We've found the cleanest claims happen when both adjusters get the same documentation at the same time, so we photograph and log damage to shared walls, attics and common corridors separately from unit-specific loss. For single-family fire claims, we work directly with most major carriers and provide the itemized scope they need to move the claim without the homeowner chasing paperwork between two offices.

## Local note

East Brunswick sits close enough to the Lawrence Brook and Farrington Lake watershed that ambient humidity stays elevated for days after a fire, even when the fire itself had nothing to do with water. That humidity slows soot off-gassing and can let smoke odor re-settle into drywall and insulation that tested dry on day one. On jobs near the brook or anywhere within a few blocks of Ireland Brook Conservation Area, we run dehumidification alongside odor treatment rather than sequencing them, because waiting for structural drying to finish first lets the smell re-anchor into porous materials.
