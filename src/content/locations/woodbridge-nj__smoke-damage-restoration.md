## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Woodbridge

We start with an assessment that separates dry smoke residue (powdery, from fast-burning fires) from wet smoke residue (sticky, from smoldering fires), because the wrong cleaning agent on the wrong type can actually push soot deeper into porous materials like drywall and grout. From there we seal off unaffected rooms, run HEPA air scrubbers to pull particulates out of circulation, and work through a cleaning sequence that moves from least invasive (dry sponging, vacuuming) to more aggressive methods (wet cleaning, thermal fogging) only where needed. Older Woodbridge homes with plaster ceilings or original trim get extra attention during this stage, since aggressive wet cleaning can damage finishes that drywall doesn't have to worry about. Odor neutralization is its own step, not an afterthought, since masking a smoke smell with fragrance never actually removes the source.
