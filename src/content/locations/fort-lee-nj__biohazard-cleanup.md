## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process

Every job starts with an assessment of what materials are affected, whether flooring, subflooring, drywall, or furnishings, and what can be safely cleaned versus what needs to be removed. Our technicians work in full personal protective equipment, establish containment barriers before any material is disturbed, and clean and disinfect affected surfaces using protocols consistent with OSHA's bloodborne pathogen standard. Sharps, needles, and other regulated items are packaged and removed through licensed medical waste handling rather than standard trash disposal. As an IICRC Certified Firm, we follow documented cleaning and disinfection procedures and provide the paperwork a property owner, insurer, or building management office may later request.
