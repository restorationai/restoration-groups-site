## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Union

We start by identifying the odor source and the materials it's penetrated rather than jumping straight to equipment. In a Battle Hill colonial with smoke odor from a stovetop fire, that might mean pulling baseboards and checking behind cabinetry for soot residue that's still feeding the smell. In a Vauxhall basement with floodwater odor, it usually means confirming the subfloor and framing are dry before any deodorization treatment goes in, since trapping moisture behind a treated surface just brings the smell back in a few weeks.

Once the source is handled, we match the method to the material. Thermal fogging works well for smoke odor that's settled into fabric, drapery, and upholstered furniture because the fog particles mimic smoke behavior and reach the same crevices. Hydroxyl deodorization is our go-to for occupied homes or spaces with pets and kids still on-site, since it breaks down odor molecules without the evacuation period ozone requires. Ozone treatment still has its place for unoccupied properties with heavy, embedded odor, sealed basements after a sewage event or a severe fire, where we need a stronger oxidizing process and the space can be vacated for the treatment window.
