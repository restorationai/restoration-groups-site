## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Piscataway

We start by identifying the source, because a sump failure, a supply line break, and river backflow all call for different containment steps before extraction even begins. Standing water comes out with truck-mounted or submersible pumps depending on depth and access, and we check subfloor and wall cavities with moisture meters rather than assuming a surface-dry slab means the framing underneath is dry too. Baseboards, drywall, and insulation that have wicked water are removed to the height the moisture actually reached, not a standard cut line, since finished basements in this area often have water travel higher into the wall than it appears from the surface. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed and monitored until moisture readings in the studs and subfloor return to normal, not just until the carpet feels dry underfoot.
