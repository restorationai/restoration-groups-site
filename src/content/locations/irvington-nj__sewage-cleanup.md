## Why Irvington Sees Recurring Sewage Backups

Much of Irvington's housing stock dates to the early 1900s, with cast-iron drain lines that have spent over a century collecting scale, root intrusion, and grease buildup. Combine that with a dense grid of multifamily rowhouses and prewar frame houses along streets like Union Avenue and Chancellor Avenue, and you get shared or closely spaced lateral connections where one neighbor's clog becomes another's basement flood. The Elizabeth River watershed runs through low-lying sections of the city, and during hard storms, municipal sewer lines can surcharge faster than aging laterals can relieve pressure, pushing wastewater backward into basement floor drains and laundry tubs. Hurricane Ida in 2021 made this dynamic painfully clear across Essex County, and the same combination of old infrastructure and heavy rainfall still produces backups during any significant storm event. Finished and semi-finished basements, a near-universal feature in Irvington's housing, mean sewage contact with carpet, drywall, and stored belongings rather than bare concrete, which raises both the health risk and the scope of what needs to be removed.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Irvington

We start by identifying the contamination category, since raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water and everything it touches gets treated accordingly. Porous materials that absorbed sewage, carpet padding, baseboards, drywall below the flood line, are removed rather than dried in place, because drying does not make blackwater-contaminated material safe to keep. We extract standing water, remove and bag contaminated debris per protocol, and apply EPA-registered antimicrobials to all affected surfaces before setting containment and negative air if the scope warrants it. In older Irvington homes near Irvington Center and Grove Park, we also check whether the backup originated from a failed check valve or a collapsed clay lateral under the yard, since that distinction determines whether a plumber needs to clear the line before we close out drying. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in the remaining structure, framing, subfloor, confirm the space is dry, not just surface-dry, which matters more in basements with limited ventilation.

## Irvington Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup claims often fall under a separate sewer backup rider rather than standard homeowners coverage, and many Irvington policies either lack this rider or cap it at a low limit. We photograph the source, the contamination path, and every item removed before disposal, and we provide an itemized scope that carriers can reference whether the claim is being handled under a backup rider or a broader water damage clause. If the backup stems from a municipal main rather than your private lateral, we note that distinction clearly, since it can affect whether the city or your insurer bears responsibility for the line itself, even though cleanup inside your home remains your claim to file.
