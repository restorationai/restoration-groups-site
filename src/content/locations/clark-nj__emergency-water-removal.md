## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in Clark

The first hour determines how much of a Clark basement finish survives. We extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable pumps, then move fast on wall cavities and subfloor, because a lot of these basements have paneling or drywall installed directly over masonry foundation walls with no air gap, which means moisture wicks upward into framing faster than it would in a newer build. We check moisture content in the slab, the sill plate, and any finished framing with meters rather than guessing from the surface, since a basement floor can read dry on top while the vapor barrier underneath is still trapping water against the concrete. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage of the space rather than a flat number of units, and we document moisture readings daily until the structure hits dry standard.
