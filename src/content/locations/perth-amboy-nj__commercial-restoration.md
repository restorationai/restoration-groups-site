## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Perth Amboy

We start with a scope that accounts for what the building is doing, not just what's wet. Extraction and containment come first, followed by moisture mapping so we know whether water has tracked into wall cavities or under original hardwood and tile that's common in Perth Amboy's older commercial buildings.
