## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Rahway

We start by identifying the contamination category and shutting down the source, whether that's a failed check valve, a collapsed lateral, or storm-driven backflow. Standing sewage and saturated materials get extracted and removed first: carpet, pad, and baseboards in finished basements almost always come out, since porous material that's contacted raw sewage can't be cleaned to a safe standard. We then clean and disinfect affected surfaces, set containment to protect the rest of the home, and dry the structure following IICRC S500 drying guidelines, with daily moisture readings until materials are back to normal range. On homes with original hardwood subfloor over the drain stack, we pay close attention to subfloor saturation that surface cleaning alone would miss.
