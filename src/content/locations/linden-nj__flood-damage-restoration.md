## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Linden

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted and portable pumps before it has time to migrate into wall cavities. In a finished Linden basement, that means pulling baseboards and, where the water line is high enough, cutting drywall a few inches above the flood mark to let the stud bays dry instead of trap moisture behind an intact wall. Cast-iron drain failures and sewer backup risk are common enough in this housing stock that we check for contamination before we treat a flood as clean-water loss.

Once extraction is done, we set air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the square footage and monitor moisture content in the framing and subfloor daily, drying to the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing by feel or smell.
