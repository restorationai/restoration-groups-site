## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Scotch Plains

We start by identifying the actual source, whether that's charred framing from a fire, sewage-contaminated subfloor, or carpet padding that held onto floodwater. Source material that can't be fully cleaned gets removed, because no amount of fogging will neutralize odor sealed inside wet insulation or scorched drywall. From there we match the method to the job: hydroxyl deodorization for occupied homes since it works without the evacuation ozone treatment requires, ozone treatment for vacant properties or sealed-off work areas where a stronger oxidizing reaction is appropriate, and thermal fogging to drive deodorizing agents into the same cracks and porous surfaces that smoke penetrated in the first place. Air scrubbing with HEPA filtration runs alongside this to pull particulate and odor-carrying moisture out of circulation while the treatment works.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Not every odor calls for the same tool. A grease fire in a Southside kitchen might need thermal fogging paired with sealant on affected cabinetry, while a basement that took on brook water near Shackamaxon is more likely to need hydroxyl generators running over several days alongside dehumidification to keep the space from re-humidifying and reactivating the smell. We size the equipment to the cubic footage and material type on site rather than running a one-size treatment, which is part of why we document moisture readings and odor source locations before and after treatment for the insurance file.
