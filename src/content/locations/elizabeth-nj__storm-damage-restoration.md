Elizabeth's building stock is part of the problem. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Elizabeth

We start with a structural safety check, because storm-damaged two- and three-family homes often have compromised rooflines or sagging ceilings from trapped water before anyone notices. Once the property is safe to enter, we extract standing water, tarp or board any open roof and window sections to stop ongoing intrusion, and pull moisture readings from flooring, framing, and the plaster or lath common in pre-1950 construction. Elizabeth's older multifamily buildings dry slower than modern drywall assemblies, so we set drying equipment based on actual readings, not a fixed schedule, and we recheck daily until materials hit a safe moisture range. Debris from downed trees or damaged siding gets cleared and staged for pickup, and we document every step for the insurance file as we go, not after the fact.
