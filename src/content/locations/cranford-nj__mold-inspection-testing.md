## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Cranford

We start with a visual assessment of the areas most likely to hold hidden moisture in this housing stock: basement sill plates, under-stair closets, and the back side of finished basement walls, which are common in homes near the Cranford Canoe Club that were renovated after a past flood. From there we use moisture meters and, where warranted, thermal imaging to map dampness behind materials without opening every wall. Air and surface sampling get sent to a third-party lab, and results typically come back within a few business days. The report identifies species where detected and gives you a clear basis for deciding whether remediation is needed, and at what scope.
