## Why Clifton Properties See Flood Damage Issues

Clifton sits low where Weasel Brook winds through town and where the Passaic River corridor near Route 21 has a long, documented history of overflow during major storms, including Irene and Ida. Homes in Lakeview and Delawanna built in the 1940s through 1960s, capes, colonials, and split-levels, were finished with basements that weren't designed for repeated water intrusion. Original cast-iron drain lines and aging supply piping in these houses are reaching the end of their service life, so a flood event often finds the weak point in plumbing that was already failing quietly. In Botany Village, the older brick multi-family buildings add another wrinkle: shared foundation walls and tighter lot spacing mean water intrusion in one unit can migrate to a neighboring one through party walls and common crawl spaces. Along the Route 3 and Route 46 retail corridors and around Styertowne Shopping Center, flat commercial roofs and aging sprinkler systems generate a different kind of loss, sudden, large-volume water events that hit overnight when no one's on site to catch them early.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Clifton

Flood water in a Clifton basement is rarely clean, and we treat it that way from the first walkthrough. After the initial inspection, we isolate standing water, extract it with truck-mounted and portable pumps, and check cast-iron and clay drain lines for backflow, which is common in the older Lakeview and Delawanna housing stock we see most often. Moisture mapping follows, because block foundation walls and finished basement framing can read dry at the surface while the cavity behind stays saturated for days. Drying equipment is placed and monitored against the IICRC S500 standard until materials hit target moisture levels, not just until the space looks dry. Where contents, flooring, or drywall were affected, we document everything for the claim before anything is removed.

## Clifton Insurance Coordination

Many Clifton homes near the Passaic and Weasel Brook floodplains carry NFIP flood policies in addition to standard homeowner's coverage, and the two don't always cover the same damage. We photograph and log affected materials by room and by water category so your adjuster, whether they're working the flood policy or the homeowner's policy, has documentation that matches what actually happened on site. For two-family and multi-family properties common in Botany Village and parts of 07013, we coordinate separately with landlords and tenants when a single flood event affects more than one unit, since each party's coverage and claim often need to be handled on their own timeline.
