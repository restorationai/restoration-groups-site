## Why Elizabeth Properties See Flood Damage Issues

Elizabeth sits low along its namesake river, and neighborhoods like Elizabethport and Bayway carry real tidal and storm-surge exposure, something Hurricane Ida made clear when the river overtopped its banks and flooded homes that had never taken on water before. Add a combined sewer system that backs up under heavy rain, and flood events here often arrive with contaminated water rather than clean runoff. The housing stock compounds it: large stretches of Elmora, Midtown, and Peterstown are early-1900s two- and three-family frame construction, built tight to the lot line with shared foundations and plumbing stacks. One flooded basement or burst riser can affect every unit in the building, and landlords need mitigation that's documented unit by unit for their own records and for each tenant's claim.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Elizabeth

We start by categorizing the water. River and storm-surge flooding is treated differently than a clean supply-line failure, and sewer backup flooding, common in low-lying blocks near Elizabethport, gets handled under sewage cleanup and sanitization protocols rather than standard extraction alone. After extraction, we check subfloor and wall cavities in the older frame buildings common across North Elizabeth and Westminster, since plaster and old-growth lumber hold moisture longer than modern materials and can read dry at the surface while still saturated underneath. Moisture mapping, targeted demolition of what can't be salvaged, and structural drying follow, with daily readings logged so the paperwork matches what actually happened inside the walls. For multi-unit buildings, we document each affected unit separately so the building owner and each tenant's carrier see a clean, individual record.

## Elizabeth Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowner and renter policies in New Jersey exclude flood water specifically, which means flooding from the Elizabeth River or storm surge in Bayway typically runs through a separate flood policy, while a burst pipe or sewer backup may fall under the regular policy's water damage or sewer backup rider. We photograph and log materials as we remove them so the claim reflects what the adjuster needs to see, whether that's a single-family loss or a three-family building with separate policies on each unit. Landlords near the port corridor and Jersey Gardens who carry commercial coverage get the same unit-by-unit documentation, which matters when a claim covers common areas and individual leases separately.

## Local note

Basements near the Port Newark-Elizabeth Marine Terminal and along Bayway take on brackish or saltwater during surge events, not just rainwater, and saltwater corrodes electrical panels, HVAC components, and metal fasteners far faster than freshwater flooding does. On those jobs we flag electrical systems for inspection before power is restored, even when the water itself has been fully extracted, because corrosion damage can show up weeks after a flood that looked straightforward on day one.
