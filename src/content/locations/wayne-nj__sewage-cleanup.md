Wayne's sewer and septic troubles trace directly to its geography. Around the lake communities, Packanack, Pines, and Lionshead, midcentury ranches and split-levels were built close to the water with finished basements that sit directly in the path of any backup, whether from a surcharged municipal line or a failing septic field. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Wayne

We start by identifying the source, backup from the municipal line, a failed septic field, or a broken lateral, because that determines whether the township sewer authority or a septic contractor needs to be looped in before we can call the line clear. Contaminated water and solids are extracted first, then every porous material the sewage touched, carpet pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation, is removed and bagged per IICRC S500 category 3 protocols, since porous materials exposed to raw sewage generally can't be saved. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected with EPA-registered antimicrobials, air movers and dehumidifiers are set to bring the space back to a safe moisture level, and we run clearance checks before calling the space ready to rebuild. In finished basements around the lake neighborhoods, that often means stripping paneling and subfloor that's been hiding moisture against the foundation for years.
