## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Parsippany

We start with a structural walk-around to flag anything a crew shouldn't step near, exposed nails, unstable framing, live wires near the roofline. Roof tarping gets a plywood or OSB perimeter first so the tarp has something solid to anchor into, then a reinforced poly sheet is stretched, batten-strapped, and sealed at the seams so wind can't get underneath and peel it back overnight. Window and door openings get exterior-grade plywood cut to the actual opening, not a generic sheet leaned against the frame, and fastened into the studs or masonry rather than just the trim. On older Mount Tabor cottages with original wood siding, we take care not to over-drive fasteners into siding that's often thinner and more brittle than modern product. Once the structure is weather-tight, we document everything with photos for your adjuster before any further work starts.
