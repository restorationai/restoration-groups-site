## Why Woodbridge Floods the Way It Does

Woodbridge Township sits low where the Rahway River and Woodbridge River cross through town, and the eastern sections near Sewaren and Port Reading also take on tidal surge during coastal storms. Ida in 2021 showed what happens when heavy rain stacks on top of an already saturated water table: finished basements in Colonia and Iselin took on water from the ground up, not just through a window well, which changes how we approach extraction and drying. Postwar capes and split-levels throughout Fords and Avenel often have cast-iron drain lines and finished lower levels added decades after the original build, so floodwater tends to get trapped behind paneling and under carpet pad where it sits and spreads bacteria long after the surface looks dry.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Woodbridge

We start by identifying whether the water is clean, gray, or black category, because river and tidal flooding is almost never clean and that determines what gets removed versus dried in place. Porous materials like carpet pad, baseboard, and lower drywall usually come out when water has backed up from the Rahway River or a storm sewer, while flooring and framing get assessed and dried to the IICRC S500 standard with extraction units and air movers sized to the square footage. In older Woodbridge Proper and Fords homes with plaster or lath-and-plaster basements, we pull moisture readings at multiple depths because surface drywall can read dry while the framing behind it is still wet, which is how hidden mold problems start. Once materials are stabilized, we document everything with photos and moisture logs for your insurance file before any flooring or drywall goes back in.

## Woodbridge Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies exclude flood water that enters from outside the home, which catches a lot of Woodbridge residents off guard after a Rahway River overflow, since that's often a separate flood policy claim rather than a standard homeowners claim. We document the source and category of water clearly so your adjuster, whether it's a flood policy or a sewer backup rider, has what's needed to process the claim without back-and-forth. For multi-family buildings near the Route 1 & 9 corridor or Woodbridge Center, we coordinate directly with property managers and document common-area versus unit-specific damage separately, since that split often determines who pays for what.
