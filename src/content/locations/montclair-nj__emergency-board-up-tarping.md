## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Montclair

We start by assessing roof pitch and material before we tarp anything, because a slate roof needs a different approach than asphalt shingle. Nailing straight through old slate cracks more tile and opens new leak paths, so we anchor tarps to the batten structure or use weighted edge systems that don't puncture the roofing itself. For broken windows, we measure to the original opening rather than guessing a standard size, since century-old colonials and Craftsman-style homes near Watchung Plaza often have window dimensions that don't match modern stock. Plywood goes up cut to fit, sealed at the edges, and secured with masonry anchors where the home has original brick or stone facade. Every opening gets photographed before it's covered, which matters for the insurance claim that almost always follows a board-up call.
