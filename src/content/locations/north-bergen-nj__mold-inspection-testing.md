## Why North Bergen Properties See Mold Issues

North Bergen is one of the most densely built towns in the state, and a lot of that density is early 20th-century multi-family stock: brick and frame two- and three-family homes stacked on hillside lots, with cast-iron drains and plumbing that predates most of the appliances now feeding into it. Overcrowded units mean more laundry, more cooking steam, and more bathroom humidity than those drain lines and ventilation systems were sized for, and that moisture load shows up as condensation on cold exterior walls and mold around window frames long before anyone notices a leak. Add in the low-lying flats near Tonnelle Avenue where storm drains back up during heavy rain, and you get basements that hold moisture in wall cavities for weeks after the street has dried out. ## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in North Bergen

We start with a visual and moisture-meter survey of the areas most likely to be compromised: basement sill plates, around window units, behind kitchen and bath walls, and any ceiling with a water stain. In rental buildings near the Guttenberg border or along Bergenline, that often means checking shared walls between units, since moisture and spores don't respect a lease line. Where we suspect hidden growth, we use a borescope to look inside wall cavities without cutting more drywall than necessary. Air samples are pulled in the suspect area and compared against an outdoor control sample taken on the same visit, and surface swabs are used when there's visible growth we need identified rather than just quantified. Samples go to an independent lab, and we walk you through the results in plain language rather than a spreadsheet of spore counts.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

A thermal camera helps us spot the temperature differential that often means moisture is trapped behind a finished surface, which is especially useful in the older plaster-and-lath construction common in North Bergen's pre-war buildings. Hygrometers and pinless moisture meters give us a non-destructive read on wall and subfloor moisture content, which matters when a property near Woodcliff or close to the Hudson River waterfront has had intermittent dampness for months rather than a single clear event. Air sampling pumps with calibrated cassettes capture spore counts for lab analysis, giving an objective number instead of a guess based on smell alone.
