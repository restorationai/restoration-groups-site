## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Scotch Plains

We start by identifying the source, whether it's surface water finding a foundation crack, a sewer lateral surcharging under street-level flooding, or a failed sump pump that couldn't keep pace. Extraction comes first: submersible pumps for standing water over an inch, truck-mounted extraction for the rest. In finished basements, that usually means pulling baseboard, cutting wet drywall at a clean line above the water mark, and removing carpet pad that's acted like a sponge against aging slab. Because so many Scotch Plains basements run older cast-iron laterals, we pay close attention to what's coming back up the drain before we close out a job, since a cleanup that ignores a partially blocked line just invites the same call again next storm. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the room, and moisture readings logged at the studs and subfloor until materials read dry, not just dry to the touch.
