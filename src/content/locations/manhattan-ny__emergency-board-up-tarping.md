**Board-up or tarping emergency in Manhattan?** We answer 24/7, and our crews travel in from Fair Lawn prepared to secure a broken storefront window, a wind-torn roof membrane, or a fire-damaged opening before the next rain gets inside. A plywood panel or a tarp sounds simple until you're forty floors up on a Midtown terrace in February wind, or trying to protect a Financial District ground-floor retail space overnight so the business doesn't lose another day of revenue.

## Why Manhattan Properties Need Fast Board-Up and Tarping

Manhattan's building stock works against you in an emergency. Pre-war co-ops on the Upper West Side and Upper East Side have parapet roofs, fire escapes, and window configurations that don't match standard lumber cuts, so a broken sash or a storm-stripped cornice often needs an on-site measurement and custom fit rather than a stock panel. Newer condo towers add their own wrinkle: curtain wall glazing and high-floor wind loads mean a single cracked pane can become a falling-glass hazard that the building's managing agent needs addressed immediately, not on the next business day. Storm surge from events like Sandy hit the Financial District and Lower East Side hard, and more recent storms like Ida overwhelmed drainage from Harlem down through Chelsea, leaving roof access hatches, bulkhead doors, and street-level openings exposed until they could be sealed. Fire damage adds another layer: a kitchen or electrical fire that blows out a window or burns through a roof section needs board-up work coordinated with FDNY clearance before anyone touches the opening.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Manhattan

We start with a site assessment that accounts for how the building actually works, not just the damage itself. In a doorman building or co-op, that means checking in with building management before we unload material, confirming freight elevator reservations, and working around service entrance hours if the loading dock has them. For roof and terrace tarping, we anchor against parapet walls and existing roof penetrations rather than puncturing membrane unnecessarily, since a patched roof deck is easier to restore later than one with new holes. Window and storefront board-up uses exterior-grade plywood cut and fitted on site, sealed at the edges to resist wind-driven rain, and marked with our company information so building staff and responding officers know the opening has already been secured. Every job gets photo documentation before and after, which matters in a city where a managing agent or insurance adjuster may not see the property in person for days.

## Manhattan Insurance & HOA Coordination

Co-op boards, condo associations, and commercial landlords all want the same three things fast: a certificate of insurance, a clear description of what was damaged and what was secured, and a timeline for follow-up work. We provide unit-by-unit documentation when a board-up affects a shared building system, like a common roof or a lobby-facing window, and we coordinate directly with resident managers and managing agents so the paperwork doesn't become a second emergency on top of the first. For ground-floor retail and restaurant spaces, where every closed day is lost income, we move as quickly as building access allows and give tenants a written record they can hand straight to their insurance carrier or landlord.
