## Equipment & Methods We Use for Smoke Damage

Smoke residue isn't uniform. Protein residue from a kitchen fire behaves differently than the dry, powdery soot from a wood-frame structure fire, and our crews adjust cleaning agents and equipment accordingly rather than running one standard process on every job. We use HEPA air scrubbers to control airborne particulate during cleanup, thermal fogging or ozone treatment for odor that has worked into porous materials, and media blasting on masonry and brick where surface cleaning alone won't lift embedded soot. Work is documented throughout so there's a clear record for your insurance carrier of what was affected and what was done.
