## Why Perth Amboy Properties See Mold Growth

Much of Downtown and the Waterfront area sits on parcels that were low-lying or filled land to begin with, which means groundwater pressure pushes into basements during nor'easters and heavy summer storms even when there's no visible flood event. Combine that with late-1800s and early-1900s construction, galvanized supply lines that weep slowly at joints, and cast-iron drain stacks prone to hairline cracks, and you get chronic low-grade moisture that mold colonizes in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Multifamily conversions common in Dunham Heights and Kearny Heights add another layer: a slow leak on one floor often shows up as a ceiling stain or musty smell a unit away, which is why tenant complaints and landlord disputes over mold are common here.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process

We start with a visual walkthrough and moisture mapping using a penetrating moisture meter on wall cavities, subfloor, and framing near any known or suspected leak point. From there we pull air samples (and surface or tape-lift samples where growth is visible) and send them to an independent lab for spore count and species identification, which keeps the testing results separate from any remediation recommendation. In older brick rowhomes near Smith Street and the Downtown corridor, we pay close attention to party walls and shared chimneys, since moisture tracks laterally through masonry in ways that don't match the floor plan. The full report gives you lab-confirmed spore counts compared to outdoor baseline air, so you know whether what you're smelling is actually elevated indoor mold or just an old-house odor.

## Equipment & Methods We Use

Indoor air quality testing only means something if it's compared against a true outdoor control sample, so every inspection includes both. We use thermal imaging to spot temperature differentials that indicate trapped moisture behind drywall or plaster without opening walls unnecessarily, which matters in century-old construction where unnecessary demolition creates its own headaches. Borescope cameras let us look inside wall cavities and under cabinetry near plumbing runs, which is often where mold starts in homes with aging galvanized or cast-iron systems. Every sample is logged with chain-of-custody documentation, which matters if the report ends up supporting an insurance claim or a landlord-tenant dispute.
