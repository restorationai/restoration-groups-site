Teaneck's housing stock works against a contained fire. ## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Teaneck

We start by identifying the smoke type, because wet smoke from a slow-burning electrical or upholstery fire behaves differently than the dry, powdery residue from a fast-burning wood fire, and each calls for a different cleaning agent. In a pre-1950 plaster home, we test a hidden section of wall before committing to a cleaning method, since aggressive solvents that work fine on painted drywall can strip old plaster finishes. HVAC ducts get inspected and, where contaminated, cleaned or sealed before the system runs again, since recirculating a few hours of soot through ductwork can undo days of surface cleaning. We seal porous materials that can't be fully restored, run air scrubbers with HEPA and carbon filtration to pull particulate and odor out of the air, and use thermal fogging or hydroxyl generators for odor that's worked into framing and subfloor. All of it is documented and dried per IICRC-referenced drying and cleaning standards, and our technicians work as an IICRC Certified Firm.
