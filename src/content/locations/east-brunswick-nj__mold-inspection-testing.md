## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in East Brunswick

We start with a visual and moisture-meter walkthrough of the areas most likely to be compromised, basement perimeter walls, crawl spaces, and the subfloor under kitchens and baths, since that's where East Brunswick's plumbing age and basement-heavy layout tends to put the problem. From there, air sampling gives us a spore count to compare an affected room against outdoor baseline air, which tells us whether what you're smelling is active growth or just stale basement air. If we find a cavity we suspect is compromised, surface or tape-lift sampling on the material itself confirms it before anyone opens a wall. Samples go to a third-party lab, and we walk you through the results in plain language, not a lab report full of genus names, so you know whether you're looking at a spot cleanup or a larger remediation scope.
