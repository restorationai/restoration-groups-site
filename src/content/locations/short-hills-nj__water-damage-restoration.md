## Why Short Hills Properties See Water Damage Issues

Large lot sizes and mature tree cover mean a lot of Short Hills homes rely on sump pumps and French drains to manage groundwater, especially on properties with partially below-grade square footage. When the power goes out during a storm, that pump stops, and water can rise several inches before anyone notices, since finished basements are often used as guest suites or media rooms rather than storage. Homes with older cast iron or galvanized supply lines are also more prone to pinhole leaks behind finished walls, which is a slower, harder-to-spot kind of water damage than a dramatic pipe burst but just as destructive to drywall and flooring over time.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Short Hills

We start with moisture mapping, not guesswork. A moisture meter and thermal camera tell us what's actually wet behind the walls and under the flooring, which matters in Short Hills homes where finished basements often have carpet over subfloor, or hardwood laid over older slab. Standing water comes out first with truck-mounted or portable extraction, depending on access. Then we set structural drying equipment, air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage, and track drying progress daily against the IICRC S500 standard rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule. If the loss involves a finished lower level with built-in cabinetry or trim, we document and photograph materials before any removal, since replacement-in-kind on custom finishes is a common point of negotiation with carriers.

## Short Hills Insurance Coordination

Most of the water losses we see in this area come through homeowners' policies with sump pump or water backup riders, since standard policies often exclude gradual seepage or sewer backup without that endorsement. We document room dimensions, moisture readings, and affected materials as we go, which gives your adjuster a clear record instead of a dispute over what was actually wet. As an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor, we can also scope and price any drywall, flooring, or trim replacement tied to the water loss directly for your carrier.
