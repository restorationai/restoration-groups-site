## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Clark

We start by finding and removing the source material, water-damaged carpet pad, swollen baseboard, saturated insulation, before running any deodorization equipment, because treating odor over a still-wet source just buys a few days of relief. For occupied Clark homes, we lean on hydroxyl generators, which produce the same odor-neutralizing reaction as ozone but are safe to run while people and pets are still in the house. For a vacant space or a sealed-off area, like a finished basement closed up after a Robinson's Branch backup, ozone treatment works faster and goes deeper into porous surfaces, but it requires the space to sit unoccupied for a set re-entry period afterward. Smoke odor from a kitchen fire or a scorched appliance gets a different approach: thermal fogging, which carries a deodorizing agent into the same microscopic cracks and HVAC pathways that smoke particles traveled, reaching spots a surface wipe-down never will.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Which method we use depends on the material and the timeline, not a one-size approach. Air scrubbers fitted with activated carbon filtration run alongside hydroxyl or ozone equipment to pull particulate and residual gas out of circulation while the deeper treatment works. We check moisture content with a meter before closing out any job, since a basement slab or sill plate that reads dry on the surface can still be holding enough moisture to regenerate odor within a few weeks.
