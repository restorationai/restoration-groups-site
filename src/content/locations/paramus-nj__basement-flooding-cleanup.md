## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Paramus

We start by identifying the water category, groundwater and storm runoff behave differently than a sewage backup from a failed cast-iron lateral, and that distinction drives everything from PPE to disposal. Extraction comes next using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on access, since many Paramus split-levels have narrow basement stairwells or walk-out doors facing a rear yard that's also sloped toward the house. Once standing water is out, we pull baseboards, test block walls and slab moisture with meters, and set containment if there's any indication of sewage contamination. Drying is calibrated to the structure: block foundation walls release moisture slowly compared to drywall-over-stud construction, so we often run equipment longer in finished basements with paneling or carpet pad over concrete than the drying charts would suggest for an above-grade room. Everything gets dried and verified per the IICRC S500 standard before we close out the job.
