## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Parsippany

We adjust the drying approach to the structure, not just the water category. A Lake Parsippany ranch with a finished basement gets moisture mapping across subfloor, drywall, and insulation cavities, because water wicks sideways through stud bays long after the visible carpet looks dry. A Mount Tabor cottage with plaster and lath needs a slower, more deliberate drying curve, since plaster holds and releases moisture much more gradually than modern drywall and rushing it risks cracking or delamination. In every case we're following IICRC S500 drying standards for monitoring and documentation, running psychrometric readings daily until materials hit dry standard, not just dry to the touch. Commercial losses in the Waterview corridor get a different equipment load entirely, usually larger desiccant units and longer-duration containment because sprinkler water tends to spread across open floor plans before anyone pulls the alarm.
