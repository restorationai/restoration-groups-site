## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Hoboken

We start with a walk-through to map how far the smoke traveled, not just where the fire burned, because in party-wall construction the visible damage almost never matches the real footprint. Porous materials (drywall, insulation, fabric, upholstered furniture) hold onto smoke odor at a molecular level, so those get removed or treated separately from hard surfaces like trim, cabinetry, and brick. Pre-1950 plaster walls common in Downtown Hoboken and Uptown absorb smoke residue more slowly than drywall but hold it longer, so we adjust cleaning agents and dwell time accordingly rather than running a one-size approach. Once surfaces are cleaned, we run air scrubbers and thermal fogging where appropriate to break down residual odor molecules before closing the job out.
