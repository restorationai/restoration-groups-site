## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Piscataway

We start with an assessment that separates dry smoke residue (from fast, hot fires, which wipes but can also grind into fabric and grout) from wet smoke residue (from slow, smoldering fires, which smears and penetrates deeper). From there we run HEPA air scrubbing to pull particulate out of circulation, seal and treat affected HVAC runs so odor doesn't recirculate through the house, and clean or discard porous materials based on how much smoke they absorbed. Non-porous surfaces get wiped and deodorized; textiles and upholstered contents either get cleaned on-site or moved out for specialized treatment. On plaster walls common in Piscataway's older tracts, we test odor penetration before deciding whether surface cleaning is enough or whether the wall cavity needs to be opened and treated directly.
