## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Linden

We start with a walkthrough to classify the smoke type, wet smoke from a slow-burning upholstery or mattress fire behaves differently than the dry, powdery residue from a fast flashover, and each calls for a different cleaning approach. Porous materials like drywall, insulation, and some flooring in these postwar homes often can't be fully restored and get documented for removal rather than cleaning. Hard surfaces, trim, and structural wood get cleaned with agents matched to the soot type, HVAC systems are inspected and, where contaminated, cleaned or have ductwork sealed, and we finish with thermal fogging or ozone/hydroxyl treatment depending on how deeply the odor has penetrated framing and subfloor. Everything is documented with photos for your insurance file as we go, not after the fact.
