## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Piscataway

We treat every sewage call as a Category 3 loss, meaning anything porous that contacted the water, carpet pad, drywall, insulation, often can't be saved and gets removed rather than dried. The process starts with containment to stop cross-contamination into unaffected rooms, then extraction of standing waste water, then removal of contaminated materials. Surfaces that remain get cleaned and sanitized with antimicrobial treatment, and we verify drying against the IICRC S500 standard before any rebuild work begins. In Society Hill's townhome and condo developments, where units share walls and sometimes plumbing stacks, we also check adjoining units for backflow risk before we close out a job.

## Piscataway Insurance & HOA Coordination

Sewage losses are documented differently than a clean water loss, carriers want photos of the contamination source, disposal records for anything removed, and sanitization confirmation before they'll release funds for materials replacement. We handle that documentation and bill most major carriers directly. For Society Hill and similar HOA communities, we also coordinate with property management before crews arrive, since many associations require notice before contractors work in shared mechanical spaces or common hallways.
