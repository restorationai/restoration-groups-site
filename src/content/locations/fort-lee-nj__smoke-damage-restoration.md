## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Fort Lee

We start with a walkthrough that separates visible soot from the odor layer underneath it, because treating only what's visible is the most common reason smoke smell returns a month later. In high-rise units, that means checking the HVAC return and any shared riser access before sealing off the space, since recirculated air is often how odor spreads to units that weren't near the fire. In the older Coytesville and Hill properties, plaster and trim typically need a sealing primer pass after cleaning, not just a wipe and deodorize, because the material itself off-gasses smoke compounds for longer than drywall does. Where contents are salvageable, our pack-out and contents restoration process moves fabric, electronics, and porous items offsite for specialized cleaning rather than treating them in place.
