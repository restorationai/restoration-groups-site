## Our Mold Remediation Process in Highland Park

We start with containment, because an open basement stairwell in a two-family home means mold spores reach the upstairs living space fast, especially in the tighter floor plans common in Triangle and Meadow. Negative air machines and physical barriers isolate the work area before anything gets disturbed. From there we remove and bag contaminated porous material, clean and treat the structural framing, and run air scrubbers through the full drying cycle rather than pulling equipment early just because a wall feels dry to the touch. Pre-war lath and plaster in North Highland Park homes releases moisture more slowly than modern drywall, so we extend drying time accordingly instead of guessing. Every job closes out with a visual and moisture-meter check before equipment comes off site.
