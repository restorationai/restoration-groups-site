Much of Elizabeth's housing stock dates to the early 1900s: narrow multifamily frame buildings with shared basements, aging risers, and plumbing that's been patched more than once. When one riser fails, it's rarely just the unit that called us, it's often the floor above and below too. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Elizabeth

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out of basements and ground floors with truck-mounted and portable units, then move to moisture mapping. In Elizabeth's older buildings that means checking plaster and lath separately from drywall, since plaster holds moisture longer and reads deceptively dry at the surface while the wall cavity behind it stays wet. We set drying equipment based on what we find, not a standard formula, and in multi-unit properties we check every unit on a shared stack, not just the one with the complaint, because water travels along bays and joists faster than it travels down a hallway.
