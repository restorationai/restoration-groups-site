## Why Scotch Plains Homes Grow Mold After Storms

Most of Scotch Plains was built out in the 1950s and 60s, split-levels, ranches, and colonials with finished basements added years after the original pour. Downtown Scotch Plains and the older streets near the Frazee House have a mix of pre- and post-war construction, which means foundation types, and how they handle groundwater, vary block by block. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Scotch Plains

We start with containment: poly sheeting and negative air pressure to keep spores from migrating into the rest of the house while we work, which matters more in split-level basements where the HVAC return is often just a few feet from the affected wall. Affected drywall, insulation, and carpet padding get removed rather than treated, since porous materials that have been wet for more than a day or two don't clean up reliably. Framing and subfloor get an antimicrobial application and are dried following IICRC S500 drying standards before we close anything back up. For finished basements in Shackamaxon and Willow Grove, where ceilings are often dropped tile over mechanicals, we pull tile sections individually so we're not guessing at how far the contamination travels.
