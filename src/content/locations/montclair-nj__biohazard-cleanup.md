## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process

We start with containment: sealing off the affected area so nothing spreads to the rest of the home while our crew suits up in proper PPE. Porous materials that have absorbed contamination, carpet, padding, sometimes subflooring or drywall, get removed rather than treated, following the same logic the OSHA bloodborne pathogen standard is built around. In Montclair's older housing stock, that often means more material comes out than it would in a newer build; plaster walls and old lath don't let contamination sit at the surface the way drywall does, so partial cleaning isn't an option. Once contaminated materials are out, we clean and disinfect remaining surfaces, run odor neutralization where needed, and arrange proper disposal of regulated waste through licensed channels. Nothing about the process is improvised, and nothing gets left for the family to handle themselves.
