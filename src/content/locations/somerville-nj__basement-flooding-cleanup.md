## Equipment & Methods We Use for Basement Flooding

Basement losses rarely behave like a flooded first floor. Water pools against foundation walls, wicks up through block and brick, and hides in wall cavities behind finished drywall. We use penetrating and non-penetrating moisture meters to track that migration, thermal imaging to spot wet pockets before they show on the surface, and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the square footage, not guessed at. For homes near the river corridor where repeat flooding is a real possibility, we also document pre-existing conditions carefully so the current loss doesn't get tangled up with damage from a prior event.
