## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Garfield

Category 3 water, the technical term for sewage-contaminated water, gets treated differently from the moment we arrive. We extract standing water first, then remove and discard porous materials that contacted the contamination, carpet, carpet pad, baseboard, and in many Garfield basements, finished drywall that was installed directly against foundation walls with no vapor break.
