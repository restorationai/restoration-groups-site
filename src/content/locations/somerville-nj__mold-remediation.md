## Why Somerville Properties See Mold Issues

Somerville's housing stock works against itself when it comes to moisture. Early-1900s Victorians and colonials around Mercer Street and East Main were built with stone or block foundations that were never meant to be finished living space, and decades of galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drains inside those same basements means slow pinhole leaks are common and often go unnoticed behind paneling or drop ceilings. Add in the borough's proximity to the Raritan River and Peters Brook, both of which pushed water into low-lying streets during Ida in 2021 and in earlier storms like Floyd and Irene, and you have basements that took on groundwater or storm runoff and were dried quickly on the surface but stayed damp in the wall cavities and subfloor. Mold doesn't need a flood to establish itself: a sump pump that struggles during heavy rain, a humid crawl space, or a slow roof leak above a Main Street storefront can all produce the 48 to 72 hour window mold needs to start colonizing drywall paper and framing.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Somerville

We start with containment, because an open basement stairwell or a shared HVAC return in one of the newer downtown apartment buildings can move spores into living space fast if the area isn't sealed first. Negative air machines and physical barriers go up before any demolition begins. From there we remove and bag affected porous materials, which in older East Main and Fairview homes often means pulling plaster or drywall back to sound studs rather than just the visibly stained section, since plaster holds moisture and releases it slowly compared to modern board. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run throughout the work to manage airborne spore count, and all surfaces get treated with an antimicrobial before we close the job out. For jobs tied to an active leak, our teams coordinate with a separate inspection step so the moisture source is confirmed fixed before remediation wraps, since remediating mold without stopping the water behind it just buys a repeat visit.

## Somerville Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Somerville tend to fall into two buckets: storm-related moisture intrusion near the Raritan River floodplain, where coverage depends heavily on the specific policy language around water source, and slow leaks from aging supply lines in pre-1950 homes, which carriers often want documented with photos of the originating leak before they'll approve remediation costs. We photograph and log the affected materials and the suspected moisture source as we go, which gives homeowners something concrete to hand to an adjuster instead of just an invoice. For condo and multi-unit buildings in the downtown redevelopment corridor, we also coordinate directly with building management when a shared wall or stack is involved, since those jobs usually need sign-off from more than one unit owner before containment can go up.
