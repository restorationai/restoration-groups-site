## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Linden

Extraction comes first, using truck-mounted or submersible pumps depending on depth, followed by moisture mapping with penetrating meters to find out how far water traveled into framing, subfloor, and wall cavities. Because so many Linden basements were finished with paneling or drywall installed directly against masonry foundation walls, we often pull baseboard and cut inspection openings low on the wall rather than assuming the surface is dry just because it looks dry. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the cubic footage of the space and adjusted as readings come in, not left running on a fixed schedule and hoped for. If sewage or groundwater contamination is present, we sanitize affected materials before drying equipment goes in, since trapping contaminants under a closed-up wall is worse than the original flood.
