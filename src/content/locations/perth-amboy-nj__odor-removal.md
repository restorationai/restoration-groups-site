Smoke that's settled into the plaster of a Dunham Heights two-family, or the sour mildew smell rising from a Waterfront basement that took on brackish water during the last nor'easter, doesn't fade on its own. Perth Amboy's mix of pre-1950 frame multifamilies and brick rowhomes holds odor differently than newer construction, and a quick spray-and-air-out approach rarely reaches what's actually trapped in the wood, plaster, and subflooring. Odor removal here means finding the source first, then treating the materials that are holding it.

## Why Perth Amboy Properties See Odor Problems

A lot of what we're called out for traces back to the city's age and its position where the Raritan River meets the bay. Older galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drains common in Downtown and Chase Avenue buildings fail gradually, and a slow drain leak behind a wall can sit for weeks before anyone notices the smell. Tidal surge events, going back to Sandy in 2012 and continuing with regular nor'easter flooding, push brackish water into basements along the lower Waterfront blocks, and that water carries organic sediment that keeps generating odor long after the floor looks dry. On the Smith Street commercial corridor, storefront and roof leaks above older restaurant spaces produce a different problem: grease, smoke, and moisture combine in ceiling cavities that are hard to access without opening finished surfaces. And because much of Perth Amboy's housing stock is multifamily or converted two- and three-family, odor from one unit's leak or kitchen fire routinely travels through shared wall cavities and HVAC chases into neighboring units.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Perth Amboy

We start with a source inspection rather than a masking treatment, because spraying an enzyme deodorizer over a hidden sewage leak or a smoldering wall cavity just delays the conversation. Once we've confirmed what's producing the odor and removed or treated the source material, the deodorization method depends on the building and whether residents are staying in place. In occupied multifamily buildings around Downtown or Dunham Heights, we lean on hydroxyl generators, which neutralize odor molecules without the ozone byproducts that make a space unsafe to occupy during treatment. For vacant units, fire-damaged spaces, or jobs where we need faster saturation into porous materials like old plaster and exposed framing, ozone treatment or thermal fogging gets the deodorizing agent into surfaces that a surface wipe-down can't reach. Thermal fogging in particular works well on the smoke residue that clings to the wood lath and joists typical of Perth Amboy's older housing stock, since the fog particle size matches the smoke particle size that caused the odor in the first place.

## Perth Amboy Insurance Coordination

Odor claims in Perth Amboy often get complicated in multifamily buildings, where a leak or fire in one unit generates an odor claim from a neighboring tenant who wasn't the policyholder. We document the odor source, the affected materials, and the treatment method with photos and moisture or air-quality readings where relevant, which gives adjusters a clear basis for approving work across shared walls, not just inside the unit of origin. As an IICRC Certified Firm, we follow recognized industry drying and odor-control standards when we write up that documentation, which tends to move claims along faster than a vague invoice line for "deodorizing."

## Local note

One thing that trips up out-of-town crews: a lot of Perth Amboy's older frame buildings near the Proprietary House and the historic downtown core have horsehair plaster walls instead of drywall. Horsehair plaster absorbs and slowly releases odor-causing moisture much like it does with water damage, so a single hydroxyl pass that would clear a drywall unit in a day or two often needs a second treatment cycle here, and we plan the job timeline around that instead of promising a one-visit fix that won't hold.
