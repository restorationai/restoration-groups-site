## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Parsippany

We start with a walkthrough to separate what's salvageable from what isn't, because soot chemistry varies by what burned. Protein residue from a kitchen fire smells and behaves differently than synthetic residue from a garage or electrical fire, and the cleaning agents have to match. Board-up and tarping come first if the structure is open to weather, which matters more here than in milder climates: a Parsippany winter storm rolling through a fire-damaged roof can turn a soot problem into a freeze and water problem within a day. From there we run HEPA air scrubbers to manage airborne particulate, hand-clean and seal affected surfaces, pull contents for pack-out and off-site cleaning when a room needs to be gutted down to the studs for odor work, and finish with thermal fogging or ozone treatment depending on how deep the smoke odor has penetrated framing and insulation.
