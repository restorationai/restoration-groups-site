## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Hillside

We start with a walkthrough to classify the soot type, dry smoke from a fast-burning fire behaves differently than the wet, greasy residue from a smoldering kitchen or upholstery fire, and that classification drives which cleaning agents and tools we use.
