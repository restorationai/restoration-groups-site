## Why Summit Properties See Mold Issues

Summit sits high enough on the Watchung ridge that river flooding isn't the threat it is for towns along the Rahway or Passaic, but elevation creates its own problem: water has to go somewhere, and on a hillside lot it runs downhill into whatever basement happens to be in the way. Original cast iron and galvanized supply lines inside plaster walls corrode from the inside out, and because the pipe is hidden behind lath and plaster instead of drywall, a slow leak can run for weeks before a stain shows up on the surface. Freeze events hit uninsulated radiator lines in old rear additions and third-floor wings particularly hard. Add in deep finished basements that homeowners near the New Providence border use as living space, playrooms, or wine storage, and you get conditions where mold can establish itself somewhere nobody walks through regularly.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Summit

We start with a visual walkthrough and moisture mapping using a moisture meter and thermal imaging to trace water intrusion back through plaster and framing rather than guessing from a visible stain. In homes with original lath-and-plaster walls, surface readings can look dry while the material underneath stays saturated for a surprising length of time, so we check multiple depths before deciding whether sampling is even necessary. When airborne or surface sampling is warranted, we collect spore trap and/or swab samples and send them to a third-party lab for analysis, keeping the inspection and any remediation recommendation separate from the lab result so you get an unbiased read. For Summit's older homes we also note where original millwork, hardwood flooring, or built-ins sit near the suspect area, since that detail matters if the report leads to a remediation scope.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

Beyond moisture meters and thermal cameras, we use a borescope to look inside wall cavities without cutting into plaster unnecessarily, which matters on century-old walls where patching is slower and pricier than on drywall. Humidity and temperature logging over a short period can help confirm whether a damp basement is a one-time event or a chronic condensation pattern tied to how the house was finished. Our team is part of an IICRC Certified Firm, and any sampling or clearance work follows recognized industry protocols so the documentation holds up if you need it for a real estate transaction, an insurance conversation, or a contractor scope.
