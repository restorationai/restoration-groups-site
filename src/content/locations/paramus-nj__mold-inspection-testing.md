## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Paramus

We start with a visual inspection of the areas most likely to hold moisture, finished basement walls, behind baseboards, around HVAC returns, and any spot where a past leak or flood was reported. From there we use a moisture meter to check framing and subfloor readings in areas that look dry at the surface but may still be holding water, since drywall and old plaster can read deceptively dry while the stud bay behind it stays wet. If conditions warrant it, we collect air samples and in some cases surface samples, which get sent to an independent lab for analysis, and we walk the property owner through what the spore counts and species mean in plain terms, not lab jargon. The goal of an inspection is a clear answer: is there a problem, where is it, and what's driving it.
