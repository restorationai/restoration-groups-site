## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Springfield

We start by identifying the odor source rather than guessing at a treatment. That often means checking crawl spaces and basement wall cavities in older Springfield center homes for residual moisture, since a smell that seems to come from "everywhere" in the house is frequently traced to one damp joist bay or a section of insulation that never fully dried after a prior flood. Once the source material is addressed, we apply the right tool for the job: hydroxyl generators for occupied spaces where people or pets are present (hydroxyl treatment doesn't produce the ozone byproducts that make a space unsafe to enter), ozone treatment for unoccupied properties with heavier smoke or sewage odor loads, and thermal fogging to drive deodorizing agents into the same pores and crevices that absorbed the original smoke or moisture. For commercial kitchens near the Route 22 strip, we'll also address duct interiors and hood systems, since grease-laden odor compounds settle there and keep reintroducing smell into the dining space long after the walls are clean.
