## Why Fort Lee Properties See Mold Issues

Fort Lee's building stock is split in a way that matters for mold work. Along the cliffside corridor near the George Washington Bridge and through Central Fort Lee, dense condo and apartment towers share plumbing risers, HVAC chases, and parking-level mechanical rooms, so a single appliance or pipe failure can push moisture laterally into neighboring units through party walls and shared flooring assemblies. Further from the river, in Linwood and the older sections of Coytesville, homes built mid-century or earlier often have original crawlspaces, minimal vapor barriers, and roof and gutter systems that weren't designed for the runoff patterns the town sees now. Fort Lee sits directly on the Palisades cliff edge, and storm water moving downhill toward the Hudson finds the low points fast, which is part of why basement and crawlspace moisture is such a persistent pattern here. After Ida in 2021, a lot of Bergen County saw just how quickly that runoff can overwhelm older drainage, and mold growth often shows up in the following weeks, not immediately.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Fort Lee

The first step is figuring out why the property has moisture in the first place, since treating the mold without solving the source just means a repeat call in a few months. In high-rise units near Hudson Lights or along Main Street, that often means coordinating with building management to trace a shared line or confirm whether neighboring units are also affected. Once the source is contained, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't spread into occupied hallways or adjacent units, a real concern in buildings with shared corridors and HVAC returns. Affected porous materials are removed per IICRC guidelines, surfaces are cleaned and treated, and air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run through the remediation period. In older Coytesville and The Hill homes, crawlspace and framing work takes longer because older lumber and masonry hold moisture differently than modern drywall assemblies, so drying times get calibrated to the actual material, not a generic schedule.

## Fort Lee HOA & Insurance Coordination

Condo and co-op boards along the Palisades corridor frequently have their own notification and access requirements before remediation work can start in a shared building, and some require documentation of containment measures before and after the job. We work directly with property managers and building engineers to get that paperwork in order so the job isn't held up waiting on a committee sign-off. On the insurance side, mold claims in Fort Lee often hinge on whether the moisture source was a sudden pipe failure (typically covered) or long-term seepage (often excluded), so documenting the cause clearly from day one matters for how a 07024 claim gets handled.
