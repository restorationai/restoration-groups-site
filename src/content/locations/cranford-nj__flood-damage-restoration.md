## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Cranford

The first move is always getting standing water out of the basement, whether that's a Sunny Acres cape with a few inches in the laundry area or a Lincoln Park East colonial with a flooded rec room. From there we moisture-map the foundation walls and framing, because older masonry reads deceptively dry at the surface while it's still saturated a few inches in. Structural drying follows with commercial air movers and dehumidification sized to the space, not guesswork, and we track moisture content daily until the materials hit a safe benchmark rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule. Every step gets photographed and logged, which matters more here than almost anywhere else we work, because Cranford homeowners are often filing a claim they've filed before.
