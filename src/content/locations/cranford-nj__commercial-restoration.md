## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Cranford

We start with moisture mapping across the affected floor plate, not just the visible wet areas, because older commercial basements in Cranford tend to have block or parged-masonry foundation walls that wick water laterally well past where it first entered. For retail and office tenants that need to stay open, we sequence the drying zone by zone instead of shutting down the whole space, which matters a lot to a shop owner on a lease near Downtown Cranford who can't afford a full week dark. Standard commercial property policies often exclude flood, which means a lot of Cranford businesses are carrying separate NFIP flood coverage or a private flood endorsement, and the paperwork for each is different.
