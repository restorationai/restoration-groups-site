Much of the city's housing stock, the two- and three-family frame houses in Uptown and Midtown, the brick rowhomes closer to Downtown Bayonne, was built before mid-century codes addressed wind uplift the way current standards do. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Bayonne

We start by securing the structure, not patching it: plywood over broken or missing windows and doors, reinforced tarping lashed and battened over roof openings so wind can't work it loose overnight. On multi-family rowhomes we check party-wall exposures, since a board-up on one unit in a Downtown Bayonne triple-decker can affect a shared wall or vestibule the neighboring tenant uses. Every opening gets photographed and measured before and after, which matters both for your insurance file and for whatever fire damage, water removal, or storm damage work follows once the property is weather-tight. We carry stock sizing for standard Bayonne window and door openings so a crew isn't improvising lumber cuts in the dark during a bad storm.
