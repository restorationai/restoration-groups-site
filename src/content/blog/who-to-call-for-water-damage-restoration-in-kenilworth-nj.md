## What Happens When You Call The Restoration Group?

When you call (855) 650-7422, you reach a live person, not a voicemail. Here is what to have ready and what happens next.

**What to tell them:**
- Your address (07033 zip for Kenilworth)
- The source of the water, if you know it (pipe, appliance, sump failure, storm backup)
- Whether the source is still active
- Whether any electrical panels or outlets are affected
- A rough sense of how much area is wet (one room, the whole basement, multiple floors)

**What happens on arrival:**

The crew performs a moisture assessment first. They use calibrated moisture meters and thermal imaging to map exactly where water has traveled, including inside walls and under flooring where you cannot see it. This is not optional: the [IICRC S500 standard](https://www.iicrc.org) requires documented moisture readings before and throughout drying to prove the structure is returned to a dry standard.

Next, they classify the water. Clean water from a supply line (Category 1) is handled differently from a washing machine overflow with detergent residue (Category 2) or a sewage backup from Kenilworth's aging clay sewer laterals (Category 3, also called black water). Category 3 requires full PPE, antimicrobial treatment, and additional disposal protocols.

Extraction comes before drying. Truck-mounted or portable extractors pull standing water out of carpet, flooring, and cavities. Then air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are positioned to drive the drying process. The crew monitors readings daily and adjusts equipment placement until the structure hits the target moisture content.

Every reading, every equipment placement, every day of drying is logged. That log is what your insurance adjuster uses to validate the claim.

## What Should I Do Before the Crew Arrives?

A few steps in the next 15-30 minutes reduce the total damage and protect your claim.

1. **Shut off the water source** if you have not already and can do so safely.
2. **Cut power to the affected area** at the breaker panel if water is near outlets, appliances, or the panel itself. If you are unsure, leave it and let the crew assess.
3. **Move valuables and electronics** off wet floors and out of the affected space if you can do so without stepping into standing water near electrical sources.
4. **Do not run fans or your HVAC** to try to dry things out. Fans spread moisture and can push contaminated air into unaffected areas. Proper drying requires calibrated equipment and controlled airflow.
5. **Take photos and video** of everything before touching anything. Wide shots of the room, close-ups of the water source, close-ups of affected flooring and walls. This is your documentation baseline.
6. **Do not discard anything yet.** Damaged materials are part of your insurance claim. Nothing leaves until the adjuster or your restoration crew has documented it.

Mold can begin developing within 24 to 48 hours of a water event, according to the [EPA's mold guidance](https://www.epa.gov/mold). Fast extraction and drying is the only way to prevent a water damage call from becoming a mold remediation call.
