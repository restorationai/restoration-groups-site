## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in East Brunswick

We start with a walkthrough to classify the smoke: dry smoke from fast, high-heat fires leaves a powdery residue that's easier to lift, while wet smoke from smoldering fires leaves a sticky film that bonds to surfaces and needs stronger solvent action. From there, we isolate the affected zones with containment barriers so soot doesn't migrate into unaffected rooms during cleaning, pull and inventory salvageable contents, and run HEPA air scrubbers to drop airborne particulate before any surface work begins. Hard surfaces get cleaned and sealed, upholstery and soft goods are either treated on-site or packed out, and we finish with thermal fogging or ozone treatment in unoccupied spaces to neutralize odor that's worked into porous materials like drywall and subfloor. Homes in Frost Woods and along Old Bridge Turnpike with original ductwork often need that supply and return system cleaned separately, since treating only the living space leaves residue sitting in the ducts to recirculate the next time the furnace kicks on.
