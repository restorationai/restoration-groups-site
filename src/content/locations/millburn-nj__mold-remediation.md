## Our Mold Remediation Process in Millburn

We start with containment, because open floor plans and shared HVAC returns common in Short Hills additions and renovated colonials can spread spores through a house fast if containment isn't sealed correctly. Crews set up negative air pressure with HEPA filtration, physically remove and bag affected porous materials rather than trying to "treat" saturated drywall or insulation, and apply antimicrobial treatment to framing and subfloor that's structurally sound but was in contact with the colony.
