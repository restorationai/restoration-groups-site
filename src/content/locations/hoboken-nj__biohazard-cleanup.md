## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Hoboken

We start by isolating the affected area with physical barriers and negative air containment so nothing travels into shared stairwells or HVAC returns, which matters enormously in party-wall construction where airflow between units is rarely as sealed as building plans suggest. Affected porous materials, flooring, drywall, and textiles that can't be fully decontaminated are removed and packaged as regulated medical waste, logged, and transported by licensed handlers, not left curbside for standard pickup. Surfaces are then cleaned and disinfected using EPA-registered hospital-grade products, and we verify the space meets accepted cleaning standards before we consider the job closed. Throughout, our crews wear full PPE and follow OSHA bloodborne pathogen protocols, and we keep vehicles and uniforms unmarked on request, which matters on a tight residential block where a liveried truck draws attention a grieving family doesn't want.
