## Why Cranford Properties See Elevated Fire Risk

A lot of Cranford homes, especially around Downtown Cranford and the Riverside Drive area, still run on electrical systems that have been patched and upgraded piecemeal over eighty or ninety years. Space heaters, sump pump circuits, and dehumidifiers running hard in damp basements put additional load on wiring that was never rated for it. ## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Cranford

We start with board-up and tarping to secure the structure, because a fire that's cracked a window or burned through roof decking near Nomahegan Park or anywhere else in town leaves the house open to weather and pests until it's sealed. From there we separate what's salvageable: soot sits very differently on the old plaster walls common in Cranford capes than it does on modern drywall, so plaster generally needs gentler mechanical cleaning or soda blasting rather than the harsher chemical sponges that work fine on newer homes. Contents that can be saved go through our pack-out process so cleaning and odor treatment happen off-site while structural drying and deodorization proceed in the house. Full basements, standard in nearly every Cranford property we touch, get their own inspection pass, since smoke and heat travel differently through a finished basement than an open crawl space.
