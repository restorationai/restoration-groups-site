## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Clifton

The first call is about stopping the source, not just cleaning up after it. We confirm the main or local shutoff is closed, then assess what the water has touched: subfloor, baseboards, drywall, and in older Clifton homes, often plaster and lath that holds moisture differently than modern board.
