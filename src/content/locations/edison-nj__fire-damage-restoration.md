## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Edison

We start with a walk-through to separate structural damage from surface soot and odor, because treating them the same way wastes time and money. Framing, subfloor and drywall get tested for char depth and moisture before anything is torn out, since a board that looks blackened on the surface may still be structurally sound underneath. From there we board up broken windows or damaged exterior walls to secure the property, run HEPA air scrubbers to pull particulate out of the air, and begin dry-sponge or chemical cleaning on surfaces based on the type of residue, protein, synthetic, or natural char each behaves differently. Odor is handled last, once the source material is removed, using thermal fogging or hydroxyl generators rather than masking sprays that wear off in a week.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Fire Damage

Soot type drives equipment choice more than most homeowners expect.
