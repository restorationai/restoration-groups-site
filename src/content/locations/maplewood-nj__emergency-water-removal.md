## Our Emergency Water Removal Process in Maplewood

We start by identifying the water category and shutting off the source if it's a supply-line or appliance failure, then move straight to extraction with truck-mounted and portable units sized for finished basements and crawl spaces alike. In homes with original plaster walls, we're careful with moisture meters because plaster reads differently than drywall: it can look dry at the surface while the lath behind it stays saturated for days. We set containment around salvageable flooring, pull baseboards where water has wicked upward, and place air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's cubic footage, not a one-size-fits-all count. Drying is documented and verified against the IICRC S500 standard so the file holds up if your insurer asks for drying logs.
