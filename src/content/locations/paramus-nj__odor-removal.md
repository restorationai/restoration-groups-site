## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Paramus

We start by identifying the actual source, not just the smell. That means checking behind baseboards and under flooring in finished basements, inspecting HVAC returns for smoke particulate buildup, and, for water-related odor, confirming whether subfloor or framing is still holding moisture with a meter before we touch anything cosmetic. Once the source is isolated, we remove or seal porous materials that can't be fully deodorized, then apply the right combination of hydroxyl treatment, ozone treatment, or thermal fogging depending on the material and whether the space is occupied. Hydroxyl generators are our go-to for occupied homes and active retail spaces because they don't require evacuation, while ozone treatment is reserved for vacant properties given off-gassing concerns. Thermal fogging works well on smoke residue that's settled into fabric, drapery, and soft goods throughout a split-level's open layout. We finish with air quality verification before calling the job closed, following IICRC-referenced odor control protocols as a certified firm (IICRC #210213).
