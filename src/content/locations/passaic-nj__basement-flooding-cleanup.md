## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Passaic

We start by identifying the water category, because a sump failure after a hard rain is treated differently than water that's picked up sewage from a backed-up lateral, which is common in the older cast-iron systems common on the east side of town. In Passaic's older rowhomes and frame two-families, we frequently find that water has wicked up into baseboard trim and the bottom course of framing even when the basement floor is bare concrete, so we don't call a basement dry just because the puddles are gone. Drying is set up per the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage and the amount of porous material involved, not a flat per-room count. Where sewage was part of the loss, we sanitize affected surfaces before drying equipment goes in, since trapping contaminated moisture behind a fan does more harm than good.
