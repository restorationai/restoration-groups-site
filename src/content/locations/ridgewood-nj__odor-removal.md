## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Ridgewood

We start by identifying the source material rather than just the smell, since a plaster wall, original hardwood, and a modern subfloor all hold odor differently and need different treatment. For smoke odor removal after a fire, we typically pair thermal fogging (which penetrates the same cracks and pores the smoke originally did) with HEPA air scrubbing to pull particulate out of the air during the job. For moisture-driven odors in basements near the brook, hydroxyl deodorization is often the better call since it works safely in occupied spaces without requiring residents to leave, unlike ozone treatment, which we reserve for vacant properties or sealed-off areas because of its reactivity with certain finishes and fabrics. Where contents are involved, textiles and upholstered furniture get treated separately rather than assumed clean once the room smells better.
