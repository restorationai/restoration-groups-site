## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Plainfield

We start by pumping standing water and tracking down the source, whether that's a surcharged storm drain, a failed sump system, or a cracked foundation wall letting groundwater in after the brooks crest. From there we moisture-map the space, which in older Plainfield homes often means checking behind plaster and lath as well as drywall, since plaster can read dry at the surface while still holding water underneath. Flooring, baseboards, and wall sections that can't be salvaged come out early to stop mold from colonizing, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours in a warm, humid basement. We set air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage and material mix, not a generic basement, because a finished basement near the Front Street business district with drywall and carpet pad dries very differently than an unfinished stone-foundation basement near Sleepy Hollow. We monitor moisture daily and document readings for your insurance file as we go.

Homes built before the 1950s near the Van Wyck Brooks Historic District and scattered through Netherwood often still run on the original cast-iron lateral line between the house and the street main.
