## Our Mold Remediation Process in Morristown

We start with containment, sealing the work area with negative air pressure so spores don't travel into HVAC returns, which matters more in Morristown's older homes where ductwork often runs through the same joist bays as the plumbing that caused the problem. From there, affected porous materials (drywall, insulation, sometimes subfloor) are removed and bagged per IICRC S520 guidelines, framing is HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and air scrubbers run until surface and air sampling come back clean. In a Victorian near Franklin Corner with plaster-and-lath walls, that process runs slower than it would in a drywall-only condo near the Green: plaster absorbs and releases moisture gradually, so drying logs often extend a few extra days even after the surface reads dry. We document every step with photos and moisture readings for your insurance file, since carriers want to see containment and clearance evidence, not just a before-and-after photo.
