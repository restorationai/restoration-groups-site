## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Bridgewater

Commercial work starts differently than a residential call. The first conversation is usually with a property manager, facilities lead or ownership group, not a single homeowner, so documentation has to satisfy multiple stakeholders at once. Our crews photograph and moisture-map the affected areas before any extraction begins, separate damaged inventory or fixtures from structural materials, and set containment to keep active tenant spaces or adjoining units operating wherever that's safe. Drying follows IICRC S500 guidelines, with monitoring logs that track moisture content day by day so a property manager has a clean record for ownership, lenders or insurance without having to ask for it. For multi-tenant buildings, we sequence work to minimize how many units go offline at once, which matters more in a strip center on Route 22 than it ever would in a single-family basement.
