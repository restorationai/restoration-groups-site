## Why East Brunswick Properties See Mold Issues

Much of East Brunswick's housing stock dates to the postwar boom, ranches, split-levels and colonials built through the 1950s to 1970s, nearly all with finished basements that were never designed with vapor barriers or modern drainage in mind. Add the Farrington Lake watershed and Lawrence Brook running through town, and you get low-lying pockets, especially near Route 18 and the older tracts east of Frost Woods, that took on water during Irene and the remnants of Ida. Original cast-iron drain lines and supply plumbing in these homes are also past their expected service life, and a slow drain backup or a hairline pipe leak behind a finished wall can feed mold growth for weeks before anyone notices the smell. Newer townhome and condo developments around Village of the Golden Triangle bring a different pattern: shared walls and HVAC systems mean a moisture problem in one unit can migrate into a neighbor's before either owner knows there's an issue.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in East Brunswick

We start with containment, sealing off the affected area with plastic sheeting and negative air pressure so spores don't travel into the rest of the home while work is underway, a step that matters more in finished basements where HVAC returns are often close to the source. From there, we remove and dispose of materials that can't be salvaged, typically water-damaged drywall, insulation, and carpet padding, then HEPA-vacuum and treat the remaining structure. Air scrubbers run throughout to manage airborne spore counts, and we use moisture meters to confirm the framing and subfloor are dry before we close anything back up. In older East Brunswick homes with plaster or original lath, drying takes longer than it would in a newer build with modern drywall, since those materials hold moisture and release it slowly.

## East Brunswick Insurance & HOA Coordination

Mold claims in East Brunswick usually trace back to a covered water event, a burst supply line, a sump pump failure, storm intrusion near the brook, rather than mold itself, which most policies treat as a secondary result rather than a standalone peril. We document the source and the damage with photos and moisture readings so the claim has a clear paper trail for your adjuster. For condo and townhome owners in developments like Village of the Golden Triangle, we also coordinate with property management or HOA boards when the loss involves shared walls, common hallways, or building-wide HVAC, since those situations often require sign-off before work can start or close out.
