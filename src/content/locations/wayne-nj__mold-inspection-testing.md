## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Wayne

We start with a visual inspection and moisture mapping, using meters and thermal imaging to find damp framing or subfloor behind finished surfaces, which matters most in the lake communities where basements are fully built out. If conditions or visible growth warrant it, we collect air samples with a spore trap to measure what's actually circulating versus outdoor baseline levels, and surface or tape samples from any suspect material. Every sample goes to an independent lab, and we walk the resulting report through with you in plain language: what was found, where, and whether it points to an active moisture source that needs to be addressed before any remediation conversation starts.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

Findings are only as good as the tools behind them. We use calibrated moisture meters and thermal cameras to locate wet material without opening walls unnecessarily, spore trap air sampling to quantify what's in the air, and surface sampling where growth is visible, all read by an independent lab rather than in-house. Sampling protocols follow IICRC industry guidance for consistency, which matters when a report needs to hold up for an insurance adjuster, a lender, or a lake community association reviewing a sale.
