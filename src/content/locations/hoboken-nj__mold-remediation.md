## Why Hoboken Properties See Mold Issues

Hoboken sits on reclaimed tidal marsh along the Hudson, and that history shows up in how water moves through the ground here. Combined sewer overflows during heavy rain, the kind the city saw during Ida in 2021, push water back into basements and garden-level units across Downtown Hoboken and the Waterfront faster than it can drain out through storm infrastructure built for a smaller, lower-density city. Add in the dense stock of 19th-century brick rowhomes and brownstones with shared party walls and below-grade living space, and you get a pattern we see constantly: moisture wicking up through old masonry and settling in wall cavities where it has no clear path to evaporate. Newer high-rise construction along the waterfront isn't immune either. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Hoboken

Every job starts with isolating the affected area, usually with poly sheeting and negative air pressure, before any material gets disturbed. That containment step matters more in a party-wall rowhome than it would in a detached single-family home, because airborne spores have a much shorter path to a neighboring unit through shared framing and utility chases. From there we remove and bag contaminated porous materials, HEPA vacuum and clean salvageable surfaces, and apply antimicrobial treatment to the affected framing. Air scrubbers run for the duration of the job, and we don't close out work in an occupied multi-unit building without a final visual and moisture check confirming the area is dry to the standard that governs the industry, the IICRC S520.

## Local note

In the pre-war rowhomes common through Uptown and Midtown Hoboken, the original plaster-and-lath walls behave very differently from the drywall in newer construction. Surface moisture readings can look dry within a day or two, but the lath behind the plaster holds water much longer, which is why we extend monitoring on these jobs rather than calling them finished off a single quick reading. Skipping that step is how mold comes back in the same spot a few months later.
