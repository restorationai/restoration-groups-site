## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in East Orange

The first visit is about stopping the water and reading the building, not just pulling out furniture. We shut off the source where possible, extract standing water, and then check what's happening behind the walls, because plaster and brick veneer common in Doddtown and Presidential Estates homes hold moisture differently than modern drywall construction. Moisture mapping with meters tells us how far the water traveled under flooring and inside wall cavities before we commit to a drying plan. Air movers and dehumidifiers go in sized to the actual cubic footage of the affected space, not a flat number, and we recheck moisture content daily rather than assuming a fixed drying window. Materials that can't be dried, like saturated insulation or swollen baseboard, get removed and documented for the claim before anything is rebuilt.
