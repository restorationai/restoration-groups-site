## Our Mold Remediation Process in Clifton

We start with containment, physical barriers and negative air pressure to keep spores from migrating into living space while work is underway, which matters more in older Clifton homes where ductwork and framing cavities are more interconnected than in newer construction. From there, affected materials (drywall, insulation, sometimes subfloor) are removed per the IICRC S520 standard, surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated, and air scrubbers run until clearance conditions are met. In basements near the flood-prone stretches of Lakeview and Delawanna, we pay particular attention to the sill plate and the band joist, areas that stay damp long after the visible slab has dried and that homeowners rarely think to check. As an IICRC Certified Firm, we document moisture readings at each stage so there's a clear record of what was wet, what was removed, and what tested clear.

## Clifton Insurance Coordination

Mold tied to a sudden event, like Ida floodwater or a burst supply line, is usually treated differently by carriers than mold from long-term neglect or chronic humidity, and the distinction matters a lot for Clifton homeowners given how many finished basements here have flooded at least once. We photograph and document moisture intrusion at each stage specifically so that distinction is defensible when a claim is filed, and we can speak directly with adjusters about what we found and why materials were removed.
