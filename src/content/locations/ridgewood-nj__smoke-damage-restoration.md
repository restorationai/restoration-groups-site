## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Ridgewood

We start with a walk-through to map how far soot traveled, which in a deep, multi-level Ridgewood colonial can mean checking finished basements, attic knee walls, and HVAC returns separately rather than treating the home as one zone.
