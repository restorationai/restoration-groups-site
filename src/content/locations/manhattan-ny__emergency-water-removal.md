## Why Manhattan Buildings Flood Differently

Most water emergencies here trace back to the building, not the weather. A failed supply riser, a corroded radiator valve, or a washing-machine hose on a high floor sends water straight down through shared walls and slab penetrations, so the worst damage often shows up one or two floors below the source. Pre-war co-ops on the Upper West Side and Upper East Side carry original cast-iron risers and plaster ceilings that hold water differently than the drywall in a newer Financial District tower, which changes how fast a ceiling needs to come down versus how long it can be dried in place. Storm-driven flooding is the other pattern: Sandy's surge reached the Financial District and Lower East Side, and Ida's rainfall overwhelmed storm drains from Harlem down through Chelsea, pushing street and sewer water into basements and ground-floor retail that have no good way to drain on their own.

## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in Manhattan

The first call is about stopping the source and protecting the units around the loss, not just the one it happened in. Once water is shut off at the riser or appliance, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable units sized for tight stairwells and service elevators, then take moisture readings through the affected ceiling, wall, and floor assemblies rather than relying on a surface check. In a building, that log has to cover every unit touched, not just the one that called, because a board or managing agent will ask for it before signing off on any ceiling opening or drywall removal. Air movers and dehumidifiers go in by unit, and we track drying progress daily until materials read dry per the IICRC S500 standard, not just dry to the touch.

## Manhattan Insurance & HOA Coordination

Co-op boards, condo associations, and managing agents run on paperwork, and a water loss doesn't get fixed faster by skipping it. We provide certificates of insurance before any crew steps into a building, document the loss unit by unit with moisture logs and photos the board or agent can forward to their insurer, and coordinate freight elevator scheduling and building access windows so the work doesn't collide with the super's schedule or another resident's move-in. For ground-floor retail and restaurant spaces, where a closed day is a lost day of revenue, we prioritize getting water out and air moving overnight so the space has a shot at reopening the next business day.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Emergency Water Removal

Extraction equipment has to match the building, not just the loss. Narrow service corridors and older elevators in pre-war buildings mean we stage smaller submersible pumps and portable extractors instead of oversized truck-mounted rigs that can't make it past the lobby. Injectidry systems let us pull moisture out of wall cavities and under hardwood without demoing every affected surface, which matters in a unit where the board wants minimal disruption to shared walls. Thermal imaging helps locate trapped water behind plaster and tile before we cut, so we open only what actually needs to come out.
