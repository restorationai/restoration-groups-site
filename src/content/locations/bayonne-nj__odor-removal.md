## Why Bayonne Properties See Odor Issues

Bayonne sits on a narrow peninsula between Newark Bay and the Kill Van Kull, and that geography keeps ambient humidity high year-round, which is exactly the condition that lets odor-causing bacteria and mold spores multiply in finished basements. A lot of the housing in Uptown and Midtown dates to the early 1900s, with original cast-iron drain lines that back up more frequently than modern PVC, especially during high tide surges. When that happens in a finished basement, sewage odor doesn't stay at the surface, it works into wall cavities, under stair stringers, and into whatever carpet pad is down there. Add in Bayonne's tight rental market, where units in Downtown Bayonne turn over fast between tenants, and you get a steady stream of cooking oil, cigarette, and pet odor jobs layered on top of whatever the previous occupant left behind.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Bayonne

We start with a source inspection, not a spray bottle. That means moisture meters and sometimes thermal imaging to find where water or sewage has traveled behind walls or under flooring, because treating the air without treating the source just means the smell comes back in a week. Once the source material, whether that's soaked insulation, contaminated carpet pad, or charred framing, is removed or treated, we move to active deodorization: hydroxyl generators when people or pets are still living in the space, ozone treatment when the property can be vacated for a sealed treatment cycle. For smoke odor that's worked into wood framing or HVAC ductwork in older balloon-framed homes, thermal fogging gets a deodorizing agent into the same cavities the smoke penetrated in the first place.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Ozone generators are effective but they're not safe to run around people, pets, or plants, so we reserve them for vacant units, commercial spaces near the Route 440 corridor, or short unoccupied windows in a schedule. Hydroxyl generators produce the same oxidizing effect without that restriction, which matters in Bayonne's dense multi-family buildings where a job in one unit can't always wait for neighbors to clear out. Thermal fogging is our go-to for fire and smoke jobs specifically, since the fog particle size matches smoke particle size closely enough to reach into stud bays, subfloor gaps, and HVAC runs that a surface cleaning never touches.
