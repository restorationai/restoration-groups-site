## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process

We start by identifying the water source, because a storm-driven basement flood and a failed supply line call for different containment before extraction even begins. Standing water comes out with truck-mounted or submersible pumps depending on depth and access, and then we move to the part most homeowners underestimate: what's happening inside the walls and under the subfloor. In pre-war Highland Park homes, that often means pulling baseboards and drilling discreet weep holes in plaster or block walls so trapped moisture has somewhere to go, since sealed masonry foundations hold water far longer than they show on the surface.

Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying, with moisture readings logged at the start and checked daily until materials return to normal range, not just until the carpet feels dry underfoot. Cast-iron and clay drain failures get flagged separately so you have documentation if a plumber's repair becomes part of your insurance claim.
