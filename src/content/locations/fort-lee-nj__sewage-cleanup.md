## Why Fort Lee Sees Sewage Backups

Much of Fort Lee's high-rise housing stock near Main Street and the Hudson Lights complex runs shared vertical stacks that serve dozens of units. When one line clogs or a grease blockage forms low in the system, water doesn't just back up into one bathroom, it can surface in multiple units stacked above or below the failure point. In Coytesville and The Hill, older prewar sewer laterals, some original to homes built well before modern PVC piping, are more prone to root intrusion and partial collapse, especially after heavy rain events like the 2021 Ida flooding that overwhelmed Bergen County's sewer capacity. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

We start by identifying the contamination category and shutting off the source where possible, whether that's a building sewer line or an in-unit fixture. Standing sewage and saturated materials are removed first: carpet, pad, and any porous material that contacted Category 3 water generally can't be saved and gets extracted and bagged for proper disposal. Hard surfaces, framing, and subfloor are then cleaned with EPA-registered antimicrobials and dried using commercial air movers and dehumidifiers, with moisture readings tracked until structural materials return to a dry standard. In stacked high-rise units, we also check the unit below for ceiling staining or odor migration through shared chases, since a backup rarely stays contained to the unit where it originated.

## Fort Lee Condo & HOA Coordination

A large share of Fort Lee's housing is condominium or co-op, which means a sewage loss often involves both the unit owner's policy and the building's master policy, plus a property management company that needs documentation before work can proceed. We photograph the affected areas, note the source and category of contamination, and provide the paperwork condo boards and adjusters typically ask for so the claim isn't held up waiting on us. For buildings with HOA-mandated vendor insurance minimums or sign-in procedures at the front desk, we coordinate directly with building management to get crews on-site without delay.
