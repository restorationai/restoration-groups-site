## Why Parsippany Properties See Sewage Backups

Parsippany-Troy Hills sits low along the Whippany River and Troy Brook, and the Lake Hiawatha section in particular took on serious water during Ida, Irene, and Floyd. When those waterways and the stormwater system surcharge during heavy rain, municipal sanitary lines in low-lying blocks can back up through basement floor drains and laundry hookups faster than a sump pump can keep pace. Separately, the ranches and split-levels built around Lake Parsippany and Powder Mill in the 1950s and 60s are reaching the end of their original cast-iron drain life, and a cracked or root-clogged lateral under the slab can send sewage up through a basement bathroom with no storm involved at all. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Parsippany

The first step is always identifying the water category correctly. Category 3 water (sewage) requires removal of porous materials it has touched, not just drying them, so affected carpet, pad, and often the bottom few inches of drywall come out rather than get dried in place. We extract standing waste water, remove contaminated materials, and then clean and disinfect the structure, framing, and slab per IICRC S500 guidelines before any drying equipment goes in. In older Parsippany basements with finished rec rooms, that often means pulling paneling or drywall along the full perimeter to check what the water wicked into behind the wall, since cast-iron failures tend to leak slowly for a while before anyone notices the smell. Air scrubbers and negative air setups run through the sanitization phase to manage odor and airborne contaminants while the structure dries.
