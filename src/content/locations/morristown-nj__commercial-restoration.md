## Why Morristown Businesses See Commercial Water and Fire Damage

Morristown's commercial footprint is a mix of two very different building eras, and each fails differently. Storefronts and offices in the Historic District and around Little Italy often sit inside 1800s and early 1900s brick structures with cast-iron drains and galvanized supply lines that were never built for today's plumbing loads; a cracked fitting behind a finished basement ceiling can run for hours before anyone notices the stain. Newer mid-rise buildings downtown and near Franklin Corner carry stacked plumbing risers serving multiple commercial units, so one failed line on an upper floor routinely becomes a multi-tenant loss by the time water reaches the ground-floor lease space. ## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Morristown

Commercial jobs move differently than a home loss because downtime has a dollar figure attached. We start with a scope walk that separates salvageable structure from what needs to come out, moisture-map the affected square footage with meters and thermal imaging, and set containment so unaffected parts of the building, and any neighboring tenants, can stay open. Extraction and structural drying follow the IICRC S500 standard, with daily moisture readings logged for your property manager or adjuster. For fire or smoke events, we handle soot and odor removal before equipment goes in, since trapped smoke residue off-gasses worse once a space is sealed and heated for drying. Every job closes with documentation built for commercial claims, not a homeowner policy: line-item photos, drying logs, and a scope summary your risk manager or broker can act on without a follow-up site visit.
