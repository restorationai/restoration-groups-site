## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold Inspection

A thermal camera and a pin-type moisture meter catch most of what's visible to instruments, but we also use a borescope to look inside wall cavities without cutting large openings, which matters in finished basements where homeowners don't want drywall opened unnecessarily. Air sampling pumps pull spore counts from inside the suspect area and from an outdoor baseline so the lab can tell you whether indoor levels are actually elevated or just reflecting what's blowing around outside that week. For crawl spaces, which are common under the older homes near Southside and downtown, we check relative humidity readings over time rather than a single snapshot, since a crawl space can read dry on a sunny afternoon and saturated after a week of rain off the Green Brook watershed.
