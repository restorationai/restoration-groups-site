## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Plainfield

We start by identifying whether the source is a municipal sewer line backup, a failed septic connection, or a plumbing stack issue inside the home, because that determines whether the city, a plumber, or we handle the repair piece. Throughout, we document everything with photos and moisture logs for your insurance file.

## Plainfield Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage varies more than most homeowners expect. We document the source of the loss clearly, since a backup caused by a municipal main is often treated differently by a carrier than one caused by a private line collapse on the homeowner's side. As an IICRC Certified Firm and an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor, we provide the documentation most adjusters ask for without you having to chase it down.
