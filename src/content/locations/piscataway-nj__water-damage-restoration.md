## Why Piscataway Properties See Water Damage Issues

The township's exposure comes from two directions. River Road and the ground around Johnson Park sit close enough to the Raritan that Ida, and before it Irene and Floyd, pushed water into finished basements and first-floor framing that had never seen moisture before. That repeat riverine exposure means saturated soil drains slowly, so even a moderate rain event after a prior flood can reintroduce moisture into a slab that looks dry on top but is still wicking underneath.

Away from the river, the issue is plumbing age. Postwar capes and split-levels through Stelton and New Market still run on original galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks, both of which corrode from the inside out and fail without warning, usually into a finished basement. Larger Society Hill townhome and condo clusters add a different pattern: shared walls and stacked plumbing mean one unit's supply line failure can travel into two or three neighboring units before anyone notices the ceiling staining. And with Rutgers' Livingston and Busch campuses nearby, we see a steady run of student-housing leaks: overflowing fixtures, failed washing machine hoses, and radiator or baseboard leaks in older residence halls and the surrounding rental stock.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Piscataway

The first visit is about reading the building, not just the water. In a Possumtown cape with a finished basement, we're checking whether the water came up through the slab (groundwater, flood-driven) or down from a fixture, because the drying plan and the insurance conversation differ sharply between the two. In a Society Hill condo, we're tracing the line back through shared walls to confirm whether neighboring units are also affected.

Extraction comes first: pumps and truck-mounted vacuums pull standing water, then we move to moisture mapping with penetrating meters and thermal imaging so we're not guessing at what's wet behind the baseboard or under the subfloor. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and desiccant dehumidifiers sized to the room's actual moisture load rather than a flat per-room count. Flood-zone basements near the Raritan often need a longer drying window because the surrounding soil stays saturated and keeps feeding moisture back into the slab even after the visible water is gone.

## Piscataway Insurance & HOA Coordination

Riverine flooding and plumbing-line water damage are usually covered under two different policies, and that split matters in Piscataway more than in most towns we serve. A burst galvanized pipe is typically a homeowners claim; water that entered from the Raritan or from surface flooding around Johnson Park often falls under a separate flood policy, assuming one is in place. We document the source and the water line on every job specifically so the claim lands with the right carrier the first time.

For Society Hill and other HOA-managed properties, we coordinate directly with property management on access, shared-wall drying, and any documentation the association requires before a unit can be reoccupied, so you're not relaying information back and forth between us and the board.
