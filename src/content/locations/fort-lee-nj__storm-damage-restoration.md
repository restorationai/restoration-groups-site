## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Fort Lee

The first call is usually about stopping active intrusion, whether that's a torn section of roofing, a blown-out window, or water finding its way through a parapet wall. We tarp and board what needs to be sealed, then move into extraction and moisture mapping. In the older housing stock around The Hill, that means checking plaster and lath separately from the drywall sections added during past updates, since the two materials hold and release moisture at different rates. In high-rise units, we trace water paths through shared walls and notify management early, since a single-unit storm loss in a stacked building almost always has implications for the units below.

Once the structure is dry and documented, we handle contents restoration and pack-out for anything that needs to move offsite during drying or repair, and we coordinate directly with the building or property manager on scheduling so elevator access and work hours don't become a second problem on top of the storm itself.
