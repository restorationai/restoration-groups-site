## Why Hackensack Properties Hold Onto Odors

Hackensack's housing stock works against quick fixes. Pre-1950 plaster and horsehair lath absorb odor compounds the way a sponge absorbs water, which means a kitchen fire or a slow pet-urine problem can smell "fixed" at the surface while the wall cavity behind it is still off-gassing weeks later. The Hackensack River's tidal influence means basements in low-lying stretches near the water take on standing moisture during heavy storms, and anything that flooded during Ida in 2021 or since has had years to build a persistent mildew smell into joists and sill plates that a dehumidifier alone won't resolve. On Prospect Avenue and in other high-rise buildings, shared mechanical systems mean odor control sometimes has to account for a neighboring unit's HVAC return, not just the unit that called us.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Hackensack

We start by tracing the odor to its actual source, whether that's charred framing behind drywall, a saturated subfloor in a Southside basement, or biological residue in an older building's HVAC trunk line. Surface cleaning and source removal come first; no deodorizing technology works well over an odor-producing material that's still in place. From there we select the method that fits the space: thermal fogging to drive deodorizing agents into the same pore structure smoke penetrated, hydroxyl generators for occupied units or buildings where ozone's stronger oxidizing action isn't appropriate, and ozone treatment for vacant spaces that can be sealed off for a set period. Each method gets matched to the material involved and whether people or pets will be in the building during treatment.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Ozone and hydroxyl treatments solve different problems and we don't default to one. Ozone is more aggressive and faster on heavy smoke or fire odor but requires the space to be unoccupied, which works well in Main Street business district storefronts between tenants or in vacant units during a turnover. Hydroxyl generators produce a gentler oxidizing reaction safe to run around people, pets, and plants, which makes them the better call in occupied Prospect Avenue apartments or homes near Hackensack University Medical Center where a unit can't sit empty. Thermal fogging rounds out the toolkit for smoke and fire odor specifically, carrying deodorizer into the same crevices, fabric fibers, and wall voids the original smoke reached.
