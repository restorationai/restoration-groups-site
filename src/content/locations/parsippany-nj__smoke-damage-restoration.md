## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Parsippany

We start with a walkthrough to classify the smoke, protein residue from a kitchen fire behaves and cleans differently than the dry, powdery soot from a wood-frame structure fire, and that classification drives everything downstream. In the older lake-area homes we vacuum and dry-sponge porous surfaces before any wet cleaning, since plaster and older paint layers can smear soot if they're hit with liquid cleaner first. HVAC systems get inspected and, where contaminated, isolated and cleaned separately so odor doesn't recirculate through the house during the rest of the job. Hard surfaces, cabinetry and trim get cleaned with agitation appropriate to the residue type, and we use HEPA air scrubbing and thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment for odor once surface cleaning is complete, not before, since masking odor over active residue just delays the real fix.
