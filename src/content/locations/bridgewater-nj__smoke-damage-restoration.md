## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Bridgewater

We start by identifying what burned (protein, wood, synthetic, or an electrical event) because each leaves a different residue that responds to different cleaning agents. Dry soot from a fast-burning wood fire gets vacuumed with HEPA equipment before anything else, since wiping it first just grinds particles into fabric and drywall. Wet, greasy residue from a slow kitchen fire needs solvent-based cleaning and often a full repaint of affected surfaces. Ductwork gets inspected and cleaned separately in homes with forced-air systems, since soot recirculated through supply vents can re-contaminate a room days after the surfaces look clean.
