By the time a homeowner smells that damp, mineral odor coming up the basement stairs, moisture may already be wicking into joists and subfloor framing above.

## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Jersey City

We start by confirming the water source and shutting it down if it's still active, then move to extraction with truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on access. Rowhouse basements with narrow bulkhead entries or tight interior stairs in the Heights and Greenville often call for smaller submersible pumps and hand-carried extraction units rather than heavier equipment that can't make the turn. In Newport and Exchange Place buildings, we coordinate with property management or the front desk for elevator and loading-dock access before equipment ever comes off the truck, since freight elevator scheduling is usually tighter than curbside parking in a rowhouse neighborhood.

Once standing water is out, we document moisture readings on framing, masonry, and any finished surfaces, then set drying equipment calibrated to the space: dehumidifiers sized for the cubic footage and air movers positioned to pull moisture out of masonry walls and sill plates, not just off the floor. Older stone foundations release moisture slowly, so we track drying with daily moisture meter readings rather than guessing based on how the surface looks or feels.
