## Why Clifton Basements Flood

Clifton sits low against two waterways, the Passaic River and Weasel Brook, and both have a history of climbing their banks. During Irene and again with Ida, water moved into low-lying homes near Route 21 and the Lakeview section faster than many sump systems could keep pace with. Outside of river overflow events, the bigger daily culprit is the housing stock itself. Much of Clifton was built in the 1940s through 1960s, capes, colonials, and split-levels with finished basements that were never designed around today's plumbing loads. Original supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks in these homes are reaching the end of their service life, and a failed joint or a cracked drain tile under a finished rec room can go unnoticed until carpet and drywall are already saturated. In Botany Village, the older brick multi-family buildings add another wrinkle: shared foundation walls and aging perimeter drains mean one unit's basement issue often shows up in the neighbor's.

## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Clifton

We start by identifying the water source, river intrusion, groundwater seepage through a cracked foundation, or an interior pipe failure, because that determines both the extraction plan and what we document for your carrier. Standing water comes out with truck-mounted extraction, then we check framing, subfloor, and insulation behind finished basement walls, since a basement that looks dry on the surface can still be holding water in the wall cavity or under laminate flooring. Clifton's finished basements, especially split-levels with carpet over concrete, often need flooring pulled back to the slab so drying equipment can reach the actual moisture. We set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's cubic footage and track moisture content daily until readings return to normal rather than guessing on a fixed schedule. Where cast-iron drains or old clay laterals are part of the picture, we flag that separately, since a cleanup can dry the space but won't fix a failing line underneath it.

## Clifton Insurance Coordination

Most Clifton basement flooding claims fall into one of two buckets: sudden pipe or appliance failure, which is typically covered, or river and groundwater flooding, which usually requires separate flood coverage rather than a standard homeowner's policy. We photograph and document the loss as we find it, note the likely source, and provide the paperwork your adjuster needs, but we'd rather tell you upfront if a loss looks like a flood-policy situation than let that surprise come later in the claims process.
