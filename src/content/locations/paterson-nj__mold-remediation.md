## Our Mold Remediation Process in Paterson

The first step is always finding the moisture source, not just the visible growth. In rental buildings around Hillcrest and Lakeview, where multiple units share plumbing chases, we check adjoining walls and the unit below or above before we touch a surface, since mold rarely respects a property line. Once the source is addressed, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't spread into living space or HVAC returns, then remove and dispose of porous materials that can't be cleaned, HEPA-vacuum and treat the remaining structure, and dry everything per the IICRC S500 standard before closing the job. On older lath-and-plaster walls, common in the Totowa Section and near Sandy Hill, drying takes longer than it would with modern drywall, since plaster releases trapped moisture slowly even after the surface feels dry.

## Paterson Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Paterson often hinge on whether the moisture source was a sudden event, like a burst supply line, or a long-term condition, like a slow roof leak or chronic basement dampness. Carriers treat those very differently, so we document the source, the affected materials, and the extent of growth with photos and moisture readings before any demolition starts. For rental properties, where a landlord and a tenant may both be filing separate claims after a backup or appliance leak, we keep documentation organized by unit so each party's insurer gets what it needs without confusion.
