**Raw sewage backing up through a floor drain or basement fixture is a biohazard call, not a mop-and-bucket job.** We answer 24/7 for sewage cleanup and sanitization throughout Perth Amboy, where the city's aging cast-iron drain lines and tidal-flat elevation near the Raritan Bay waterfront make sewer backups a recurring call, especially after heavy rain pushes the municipal system past capacity.

## Why Perth Amboy Properties See Sewage Backups

Perth Amboy sits at the point where the Raritan River empties into Raritan Bay, and a lot of the city's housing stock, especially the two- and three-family frame houses and brick rowhomes around Downtown and Dunham Heights, was built in the late 1800s and early 1900s on combined or aging sewer laterals. Cast-iron drain pipe from that era corrodes and cracks from the inside out, and tree roots find their way into the joints over decades. When a hard summer thunderstorm or a nor'easter hits, municipal lines that were never sized for today's volume can surcharge, sending wastewater back up through basement floor drains and lower-level fixtures instead of out to the bay. Multifamily buildings compound the problem: a clog or break on one unit's line can send sewage into a shared basement serving several tenants, which is why we see a steady pattern of multi-unit backup calls in denser blocks near Chase Avenue and the Waterfront. Low-lying streets close to the old ferry slip and City Hall also carry a higher water table, so groundwater infiltration into cracked sewer laterals is common even outside of named storms.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Perth Amboy

Category 3 water, which is what raw sewage is classified as, carries bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens that require different handling than a clean-water loss. Our crews arrive in full PPE and start by containing the affected area to stop cross-contamination into unaffected rooms, a step that matters more in Perth Amboy's older rowhomes where floor plans are tight and a finished basement often sits directly below living space. We extract standing wastewater, remove and discard porous materials that have absorbed contaminated water (carpet pad, baseboard, drywall below the flood line), and dispose of them per local waste handling requirements. Hard surfaces and structural framing are then cleaned and sanitized with EPA-registered antimicrobials, and the space is dried and monitored to the IICRC S500 water damage standard before we close the job out. On properties with cast-iron or clay laterals, we flag visible pipe condition for the homeowner so they can loop in a plumber before the next backup, since sanitizing a space doesn't fix a failing line underneath it.

## Perth Amboy Insurance Coordination for Sewage Losses

Standard homeowner's policies often exclude sewer backup unless a specific rider was added, which trips up a lot of owners in Perth Amboy's older multifamily buildings where that coverage gap gets discovered only after a loss. We document the source of the backup, the extent of contamination, and the materials removed with photos and moisture readings, which is the paperwork most adjusters need to process a sewer backup claim or confirm it falls outside the policy. For rental and multi-unit properties around 08861 and 08862, we also coordinate directly with property managers so tenant units and shared basement spaces get addressed on the same timeline, since a backup in one unit's line frequently affects the building below it.
