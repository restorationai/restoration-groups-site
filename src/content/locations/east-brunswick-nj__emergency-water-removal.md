## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in East Brunswick

We start with extraction, truck-mounted pumps and submersible units for anything beyond an inch or two of standing water, because speed here determines whether a finished basement dries out or gets gutted. Once bulk water is gone, we pull moisture readings from subfloor, framing and drywall to map what's actually wet versus what looks dry at the surface, a distinction that matters in older Farrington and Tanglewood homes where plaster or older drywall assemblies hold moisture longer than they show it. Air movers and dehumidifiers go in next, sized to the affected square footage and run against IICRC S500 drying benchmarks rather than pulled on a fixed schedule, and we document moisture content daily until materials read dry, not just damp-to-the-touch.
