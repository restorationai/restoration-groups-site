## Why Water Moves Fast Through Union Homes

A finished basement is the worst place for water to sit. Carpet pad wicks it sideways under walls, paneling swells from the bottom up, and the subfloor underneath vinyl or laminate starts to delaminate within hours, not days. In Union's older colonials and split-levels, the original cast-iron drain lines are also a common culprit: a partial clog upstairs can push sewage-tinged water back through a basement floor drain long before a resident hears anything unusual. Homes in Vauxhall and the lower stretches near Morris Avenue carry the added risk of groundwater intrusion during heavy rain, which means the water showing up in a basement isn't always from a pipe at all.
