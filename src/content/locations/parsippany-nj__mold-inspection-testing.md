## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Parsippany

We start with a visual and moisture-meter walkthrough of the areas most likely to hold hidden damage, basement rim joists, finished lower levels near the lake tracts, and crawl spaces in the Mount Tabor Historic District homes where ventilation is often original to the house. Testing is a diagnostic step; if the report shows remediation is needed, that's a separate, clearly scoped project so you're never paying for inspection and cleanup as one blurred line item.
