## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Clark

We start by identifying the soot type, dry smoke from a fast-burning wood or paper fire, wet smoke from a smoldering furniture or plastics fire, or protein residue from a kitchen loss, because each one calls for a different cleaning agent. Dry soot gets HEPA vacuumed and dry-sponged before any liquid touches the surface, since water on dry soot just smears it deeper into plaster or paneling. Wet and protein residues need degreasing agents and, often, sealing and repainting once the surface is clean. Ductwork gets inspected and cleaned separately, since a Clark ranch's single-zone HVAC system is exactly the kind of setup that redistributes odor back through the house if the return vents aren't addressed. We finish with thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment depending on how deep the odor has traveled into wood grain and fabric, then verify the space reads clean before we call the job done.
