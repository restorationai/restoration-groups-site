## Why Morristown Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

Morristown's housing stock works against aging plumbing in a specific way. The Victorians, colonials, and brick multifamily buildings near The Green and through the Historic District were built with galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drains that are now well past their intended service life. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing the interior diameter until a weak point finally gives, often at a joint or an elbow fitting you can't see without opening the wall. Add a hard freeze, and a pipe that's been quietly thinning for decades can split in a matter of hours. Newer construction isn't immune either: the luxury apartment towers downtown and the mid-rise buildings off Speedwell Avenue rely on shared supply risers, and a single coupling failure on an upper floor can send water through multiple units before building management even gets a call.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Morristown

The first move is always locating and shutting off the source, whether that's a main shutoff in a century-old basement or a riser valve in a mechanical room. From there we extract standing water, pull baseboards and flooring where needed to check for trapped moisture, and set drying equipment calibrated to the structure. In the older homes near Little Italy and Franklin Corner, that often means plaster and lath walls that hold moisture longer than drywall, so we monitor with moisture meters rather than guessing on a timeline. Once materials read dry to IICRC S500 standards, we handle the repair work on the damaged section of pipe and close out documentation for your insurance carrier.

## Morristown Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental pipe bursts, though coverage can get complicated fast in condo and co-op buildings where the association's master policy and your individual unit policy both come into play. This shows up often in the downtown towers and in some of the converted multifamily buildings in Cauldwell, where water from a burst line in one unit travels down through ceilings and walls into neighbors below. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings as we go, which gives your adjuster and, where applicable, the HOA's property manager a clear record of what happened and what it took to dry it out.
