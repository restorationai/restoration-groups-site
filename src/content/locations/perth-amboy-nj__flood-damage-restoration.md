## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Perth Amboy

We start with extraction and a moisture map of the structure, since plaster and old-growth lumber hold water in ways drywall doesn't, readings that look dry at the surface can still be saturated two inches into the wall assembly. Standing water gets pumped and extracted first, then we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's actual moisture load rather than a generic equipment count. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying, with daily monitoring until materials hit dry-standard targets rather than a fixed number of days. Where cast-iron or galvanized lines contributed to the loss, we document the failure point for your contractor or adjuster before any wall or floor material goes back. Antimicrobial treatment is applied to framing and subfloor as needed once materials are confirmed dry enough to proceed.

## Perth Amboy Insurance Coordination

Flood losses here split into two very different claim paths. Storm surge and overland flooding from Raritan Bay typically falls under a separate flood policy (NFIP or private flood coverage) rather than a standard homeowners policy, while a burst galvanized supply line or failed cast-iron drain is usually a covered water-damage peril under the homeowner's policy itself. We photograph and document which type of loss you have before demolition starts, because that distinction determines which policy pays and how the claim gets filed. For multifamily properties near Kearny Heights and the Waterfront where tenant units are involved, we also document unit-by-unit damage separately so each claim stands on its own.
