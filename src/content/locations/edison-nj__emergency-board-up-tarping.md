## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Edison

We start with a walk of the exterior to map every point of entry, not just the obvious broken window or torn shingle section, because secondary openings from the same storm or fire get missed if the crew only looks at the damage that triggered the call. Plywood is cut and fastened to framing, not just to drywall or siding, so panels hold through follow-on weather and don't become a second hazard. Roof tarping uses a grid of furring strips over the tarp edges, screwed into the deck rather than just stapled, since a loose edge on an Edison roof during a nor'easter will shred within hours. For fire-damaged structures we coordinate board-up timing with the local fire marshal's clearance so the opening isn't sealed before an inspection that's still pending. Every job is photographed before and after for the insurance file.
