## Our Emergency Water Removal Process in Wayne

We start by identifying the water category and shutting off the source, whether that's a supply line, a sump failure, or river intrusion. Submersible pumps handle bulk standing water first, then truck-mounted extraction units pull what's left from carpet, pad, and hard flooring. In finished lake-community basements, we pull baseboards and float drywall to check for wicking before it's visible at the surface. Moisture meters and thermal imaging confirm what's still wet behind walls and under flooring, because a surface that reads dry can hide saturated lath or subfloor underneath. Once extraction is complete, we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's size and material mix, and we document moisture readings daily so the drying log supports the insurance claim.
