## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Woodbridge

Commercial jobs move differently than residential ones because a business is usually trying to stay open or reopen fast. We start with a scope walk that separates structural drying from contents and equipment, then set priorities with the property manager or owner around which areas need to come back online first, a retail floor before a back office, for example. Extraction and drying follow the IICRC S500 standard for structural materials, with moisture mapping on slab, block, and drywall assemblies typical of Woodbridge's commercial stock. For properties with suspended ceilings or raised flooring, common in office space around Iselin and the office parks off Route 1, we isolate affected zones so the rest of the building can keep functioning during drying.

## Woodbridge Insurance & Code Coordination

Commercial claims in Woodbridge usually involve more than one policy, property coverage, business interruption, and sometimes a tenant's own contents policy.
