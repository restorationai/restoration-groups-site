## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Livingston

We start by identifying the water category and the structural materials involved, because a copper pipe failure behind 1960s plaster dries differently than storm water intruding on a finished basement subfloor. Extraction comes first, using truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how much water is standing and how accessible the area is. From there we set drying equipment calibrated to the materials on site: older homes with plaster and lath hold moisture longer and need slower, monitored drying, while newer finished basements with engineered flooring and spray foam insulation can trap moisture in ways that aren't visible from the surface. We track moisture readings daily and don't pull equipment until the materials read dry, not just when the surface looks dry, which is a common mistake that leads to mold calls a few weeks later.
