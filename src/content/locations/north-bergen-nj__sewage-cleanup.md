When municipal storm and sanitary lines back up together, wastewater has nowhere to go but back through the lowest drain in the building, usually a basement floor drain or laundry hookup. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in North Bergen

Category 3 water, which is what raw sewage is classified as, is treated differently from a clean-water loss from the first minute on site. We isolate the affected area, shut off any compromised plumbing, and extract standing wastewater before it wicks further into subfloor and framing. Porous materials that have been in direct contact, carpet, pad, baseboard, drywall below the flood line, are removed rather than dried, because they can't be reliably sanitized once sewage has soaked in. Remaining structural materials are cleaned and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, then dried and monitored per the IICRC S500 standard before we close out the job. In older North Bergen buildings with cast-iron stacks, we also check for repeat-backup risk, since a cleaned basement means little if the same clogged lateral sends water back in two weeks later.
