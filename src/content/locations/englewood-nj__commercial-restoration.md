## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Englewood

Commercial jobs start with scope, not just damage. We document affected square footage, identify shared building systems (HVAC, electrical, water mains) that might carry the problem beyond the original space, and separate what's salvageable from what needs to come out. Drying and mitigation are run to the IICRC S500 standard, with moisture mapping that accounts for the building's construction, since a Downtown brick storefront with a concrete slab dries very differently than a Highwood strip-mall unit with a crawl space underneath. We coordinate with property managers and, where multiple tenants are affected, keep a shared log so each tenant's insurance carrier gets accurate, separate documentation rather than one blended claim. Throughout the job we try to keep as much of the business operating as possible, sectioning off work areas with containment rather than closing an entire floor when the damage doesn't require it.
