## Our Emergency Water Removal Process in Montclair

We start by identifying the source and shutting it down, whether that's a burst supply line, a failed sump, or backed-up municipal flow. From there we extract standing water with truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how the property is laid out, since a lot of Montclair's older homes have narrow basement stairwells and tight utility rooms that limit equipment access. Once bulk water is out, we document moisture levels in the plaster, subfloor, and framing, because in a pre-1950s build that reading often looks drier on the surface than it actually is underneath. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed to account for how slowly old plaster releases moisture compared to drywall, and we recheck daily until materials read dry, not just damp-to-the-touch.
