## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in West Orange

We start by identifying the water's source and category, because a sump pump overflow behaves differently than river-fed flash flooding or a sewer backup, and each dictates how we handle extraction and disposal. Submersible pumps and truck-mounted extraction remove standing water first, then we pull baseboards, carpet pad, and drywall where saturation has wicked above the flood line, which on a finished Pleasantdale basement is often higher than homeowners expect once we open the wall cavity. Moisture mapping with penetrating meters tells us how deep the water traveled into subfloor and framing, since older Llewellyn Park stone foundations and the plaster-and-lath construction common in the 1920s Tudors around Gregory both hold moisture longer than modern drywall and release it slowly. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage and materials affected rather than a flat per-room count, and we document moisture readings daily until the structure reads dry.
