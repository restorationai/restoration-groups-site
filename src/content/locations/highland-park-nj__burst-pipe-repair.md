Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing the channel until a pinhole becomes a split, often at a joint behind a finished basement wall where nobody sees it coming. In North Highland Park and the Livingston Manor section, we see this pattern most in homes that haven't had a plumbing update since the original build. ## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Highland Park

The first call is about stopping the source, so we confirm whether the main shutoff has been closed before we're even on site, and we walk homeowners through locating it if not. Once water is isolated, we extract standing water, pull baseboards and flooring where needed to check for trapped moisture, and use moisture meters to map how far the water traveled, since in older homes with plaster and lath, surface-dry doesn't mean subsurface-dry. We handle the pipe repair itself alongside the water mitigation rather than handing that piece to a separate plumber, then set drying equipment and monitor until materials read dry per IICRC S500 guidelines. For basements near Donaldson Park and the low-lying blocks closer to the Raritan River, we pay particular attention to foundation walls that may already carry residual dampness from past river-adjacent flooding, since that moisture history changes how a structure dries after a fresh pipe event.
