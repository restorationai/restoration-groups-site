## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Bloomfield

We start with moisture mapping, not guesswork: a thermal camera and penetrating meter tell us whether water has tracked under finished basement flooring or climbed into plaster lath in a pre-1930s colonial, where it hides longer than it would behind modern drywall. Extraction comes first, pulling standing water with truck-mount or portable units depending on basement access, which matters on Bloomfield's narrower lots near Bloomfield Center and the Broad Street business district where a truck-mount hose run is sometimes the only option. Structural drying follows, dried and documented per the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and desiccant dehumidifiers placed to account for how slowly older plaster and masonry foundation walls release moisture compared to newer construction. We track moisture content daily until materials hit dry standard, not until the water is simply gone from the surface.
