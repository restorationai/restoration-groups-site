## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in West Orange

We start with a damage assessment that separates what's structurally sound from what isn't, because in a pre-1950 home that distinction isn't always obvious from a walkthrough. Plaster can look intact while the lath behind it has charred, and smoke travels through balloon-frame wall cavities common in the Gregory and St. Cloud sections far faster than it does in newer platform-framed construction. From there we board up broken windows and compromised openings, shut down and isolate any contaminated HVAC runs, and begin removing charred materials, soot-contaminated drywall, and smoke-saturated insulation. Contents that can be saved, furniture, electronics, textiles, go through our pack-out and off-site restoration process rather than sitting in a smoke-damaged house while repairs happen. Odor removal is its own phase, not an afterthought: synthetic smoke residues from melted plastics and finishes behave differently than the drier, protein-based residue left by a grease fire, and we select ozone, hydroxyl, or thermal fogging treatment based on which one we're dealing with.
