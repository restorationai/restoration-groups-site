## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Linden

Every call starts with containment. Raw sewage carries category 3 contamination, and the first job on site is stopping the spread, not just mopping up visible water. We extract standing wastewater, remove porous materials that can't be sanitized (carpet pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation), and treat remaining structural surfaces with EPA-registered antimicrobials appropriate for category 3 loss. In Linden's finished basements, that often means pulling baseboards and a controlled portion of drywall to check what the cast-iron drain let through behind the wall before the backup was visible. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed based on moisture readings, not guesswork, since a basement near Tremley Point with high water table pressure will dry differently than one up near the Roselle Park border. We re-test before any rebuild work begins to confirm surfaces are safe to close back up.
