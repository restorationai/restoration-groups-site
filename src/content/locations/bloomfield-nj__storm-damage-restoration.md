## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Bloomfield

The first call is about keeping water and wind out, not drying anything yet. We board exposed openings and tarp damaged roof sections, especially on the older slate and asphalt roofs common in the Bloomfield Green Historic District, where matching materials and avoiding further damage to original trim matters. Once the structure is secured, we extract standing water from basements and crawl spaces, check whether the loss is clean rainwater or backed-up sewage (a real risk in low-lying stretches near the Third River), and set drying equipment calibrated to the building.
