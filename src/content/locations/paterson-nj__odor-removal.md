## Why Paterson Properties Hold Onto Odors

Paterson's housing stock works against quick odor resolution. The city's 19th-century mill-worker rowhomes and converted silk-era industrial buildings were built with materials that absorb and hold moisture and smoke residue: horsehair plaster, unfinished brick, old joists with decades of prior water intrusion already soaked in. When a pipe backs up in a Lakeview two-family or a kitchen fire scorches a unit in a converted mill building near downtown, the odor molecules bind to those surfaces instead of sitting on top of them. Surface cleaning and air fresheners cover the smell for a day or two and then it comes back, usually worse once humidity rises. Overcrowded rental buildings in Eastside and Hillcrest compound the problem: shared HVAC runs and thin party walls mean an odor that starts in one unit's bathroom or basement storage area migrates to neighbors before anyone calls for help.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Paterson

We start by identifying the odor source and the material it's bound to, since treating a sewage backup odor in a cast-iron drain system is different from treating smoke residue on plaster. For protein and smoke odors we use thermal fogging to drive a deodorizing agent into the same cracks and pores the smoke penetrated. For broader structural odors, especially after flood events near the Great Falls area where river water mixed with sediment and sewage, we use hydroxyl deodorization, which works at normal occupied temperatures and doesn't require residents to vacate the way ozone treatment does. Ozone generators still have a place for sealed, unoccupied spaces, vacant units, storage rooms, vehicles, where we can run a controlled cycle and ventilate fully before anyone re-enters. In Paterson's older three-family frame houses, we pay close attention to wall cavities and subfloor voids, since knob-and-tube remnants and cast-iron drain lines common in this housing stock create hidden channels where odor-causing moisture collects long after the visible mess is gone.
