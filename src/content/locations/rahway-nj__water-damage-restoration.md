## Why Rahway Properties See Water Damage Issues

Rahway's water problems start with its geography. The river cuts through the middle of the city, and homes in the 07065 zip code closest to Downtown Rahway and Milton Lake sit on ground that was never far from the water table to begin with. After a heavy storm, that water table pushes up, and basements that are perfectly dry nine months out of the year take on water through floor drains and foundation cracks before the river ever crests its banks.

The housing stock adds its own risk. Victorian-era and 1920s homes near downtown and Central Rahway were built with cast-iron drain lines and original supply piping that are now past their intended service life, and corrosion or root intrusion can turn a slow drip into a flooded finished basement overnight. Postwar capes and colonials further out toward the Clark border fare a little better structurally, but many of those basements were finished decades ago without a vapor barrier, so a leak behaves differently there too; drywall and carpet pad wick water fast, and mold can start colonizing within 24 to 48 hours if the space isn't dried properly. On top of that, the transit-oriented development around Rahway Train Station and the Main Street business district has brought in multi-unit buildings and ground-floor commercial space, which means sprinkler discharges and shared-wall leaks are now part of the caseload alongside residential losses.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Rahway

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mount and portable units depending on how the building is accessed, then move into moisture mapping to find out how far water has traveled behind walls and under flooring. In Rahway's older homes, that mapping step matters more than usual: plaster and lath absorb and release moisture far more slowly than drywall, so a wall that reads dry at the surface can still be saturated a few inches in. We set up air movers and dehumidifiers and track drying progress against the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing by feel, adjusting equipment placement as readings change over the following days.

Where basements have cast-iron drain lines, we also run a camera inspection before we close anything back up, because a line that backed up once under storm load is likely to do it again if the root cause isn't addressed. That's a step we weight more heavily in Hazelwood and Leesville, where older drain infrastructure is common, than we would in a newer build elsewhere in the county.

## Insurance Coordination for Rahway Homeowners

One thing that trips up a lot of Rahway homeowners after a river-related flood is the gap between a standard homeowners policy and NFIP flood coverage. Water that rises from the Rahway River itself is typically a flood claim, while water from a burst pipe or a backed-up drain is usually covered under the regular policy, and the paperwork and adjuster process differ for each. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings regardless of which type of claim it turns out to be, and for condo and multi-unit buildings near the train station, we coordinate directly with the association's property manager since common-area plumbing and unit-level damage often get split between policies.
