## Why Passaic Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Passaic is one of the densest small cities in New Jersey, and its building stock reflects that: early 20th-century brick walk-ups, frame two- and three-family houses, and older rowhomes packed tightly from Downtown Passaic out toward the Prospect Park border. Many of these structures still carry original cast-iron drain lines and knob-and-tube wiring remnants that were never built to handle wind-driven rain intrusion or sudden flooding. When a storm pushes water through a roofline or foundation crack in one of these older buildings, it tends to travel along old electrical runs and plumbing chases rather than pooling in one obvious spot, which makes the damage harder to spot and easier to underestimate.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Passaic

We start with a safety assessment: downed power lines, compromised roof structures, and standing water near electrical panels all get addressed before anything else. If the roof or windows are breached, we move to emergency board-up and tarping to stop further water intrusion while the weather clears. From there, extraction equipment removes standing water, and if the loss involved sewer backup (common in the Third Ward and Fourth Ward during heavy rain events), we treat and sanitize affected areas rather than just drying them. Structural drying follows IICRC S500 guidelines, with moisture mapping to track how far water traveled into plaster, subfloor, and framing before closing out the job. Contents that can be saved are documented and either dried in place or moved for off-site pack-out when a space needs to be gutted down to the studs.

## Passaic Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner and landlord policies in Passaic draw a hard line between wind damage and flood damage, and that distinction matters a lot given the city's river exposure. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings that separate wind-driven roof damage from river or groundwater flooding, which helps when a claim touches both a standard homeowner's policy and a separate flood policy. For multi-family and rental properties, which make up a large share of Passaic's housing stock, we also document pre-existing conditions versus storm-caused damage, since overcrowded rental units often already show some wear that an adjuster will want distinguished from new loss.
